INTRODUCTION

Healing is not merely the mending of broken tissue or the channeling of the Force; it is the profound act of understanding. A body cannot be treated in isolation from the mind, the spirit, or the culture it belongs to. Misunderstanding a species' biology can kill them just as quickly as a blaster bolt, and ignoring their cultural needs can wound a spirit beyond what any kolto can reach.

This compendium is a living record. It is a collection of biology, cultural nuances, medical notes, and firsthand interviews, gathered through months of dedicated study, late nights in the Archives, and fieldwork.

Before this journey began, I thought I understood what it meant to be a healer. I was wrong. The seed for this entire archive was planted by Padawan Oowyn Warrel, who asked me a question: "How can you heal someone if you don't know who they are?" That singular question changed the course of my training and became the compass by which I now navigate my path. For that wisdom, she has my eternal gratitude and my honorary mention.

This work, however, is not mine alone. It is a tapestry woven from the generosity of countless beings. I must extend my deepest thanks to every patient who trusted me enough to let me learn from them, to every Knight and fellow healer who offered their medical insights and corrections, and to my friends, who patiently shared the stories, strengths, and vulnerabilities of their people so that others might be treated with the dignity they deserve.

Knowledge is not meant to be hoarded; it is meant to be shared. This archive is an offering, compiled in the hope that we might better understand one another, so that we may better heal all beings.

— Padawan Eluna

ARKANIAN

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Arkanians are a near-Human species from the frigid world of Arkania . They are bipedal humanoids with pale skin tones, white hair, and stark white eyes that lack a visible iris or pupil . A key feature is their hands, which possess four clawed digits: three fingers and a thumb . Their evolution on a dark, cold planet grants them the ability to see into the infrared spectrum, which causes light sensitivity off-world . They frequently enhance their bodies with cybernetics and genetic manipulation to improve themselves .  

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Arkanian society operates under a rigid caste system that values pure bloodlines and sharp, logical thinking . Their culture is defined by a deep-seated arrogance; they consider themselves superior to all other species and the pinnacle of evolution . Knowledge and research are highly prized, making them skilled scientists and academics, but they are also willing to profit from their advancements, often selling medical treatments or even biological weapons  

HISTORY

Arkanian history is complex, with their homeworld of Arkania having aligned with both the Republic and the Sith Empire at different times . By the time of the Mandalorian Wars, their long history of genetic experimentation had split the species into many sub-species . One of their most infamous acts was the creation of the Quermian species from the Xexto around 17,000 BBY . Their society later faced the Arkanian Revolution around 50 BBY, a conflict over their genetic experiments on the Yaka species. 

CULTURAL NOTES

Arkanian Offshoots: Genetically engineered subspecies, created to serve specific tasks for their pure-blooded creators . They are often seen as inferior, tools, or slaves, and are not considered "true" Arkanians.

Galactic Reputation: Known galaxy-wide for their arrogance and unsettling genetic research, which has horrified many other species .

CRITICAL ORGANS

Eyes (Pale white, infrared vision):  Highly sensitive to bright light and heat. Protect from sudden intense illumination or extreme heat sources. May require protective lenses or blinders in non-Arkanian environments. Do not shine bright examination lights directly into their eyes.

Clawed Digits (Hands):  Four clawed digits on each hand. Protect from damage during restraint or examination. Claws may be sharp.

Brain:  Arkanians have exceptional cognitive abilities; even the slowest can memorize entire libraries. Monitor for signs of cognitive impairment or head injury as you would for any near-Human.

COMMON DISEASES

Genetic Instability / Degeneration:  Due to millennia of genetic manipulation and the creation of "offshoot" sub-species, some Arkanians may suffer from unforeseen genetic disorders or complications.

Off-World Visual Distress (Photophobia):  Their infrared-adapted eyes are sensitive to the brighter and hotter suns of other worlds, which can cause pain, disorientation, and even temporary blindness.

Cyberware Rejection:  As it is common for Arkanians to enhance themselves with cybernetics, they may suffer from implant rejection or infection.

Sensory Overload:  Their exceptional mental capacity and sensitivity to infrared can lead to sensory overload in high-stimulus environments.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Visual Sensitivity Protocol: When examining an Arkanian, use dimmed or red-shifted lighting in the medical bay. Avoid bright surgical lights directly on the face. If the patient wears blinders, only remove them with explicit permission and in a controlled, low-light environment.

Genetic Assessment:  Before any surgery or major medical treatment, consider a thorough genetic screening. Their unique biology may react differently to standard medications or require specific dosages.

Cyberware Management:  Assess any cybernetic implants for signs of rejection, infection, or malfunction. Standard mechanical and medical maintenance protocols apply.

Mental Health Support:  Arkanians place great value on knowledge and intellect. They may not be forthcoming about cognitive decline or mental health struggles due to their culture's emphasis on perfection.

BALOSAR

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Balosars are a near-Human sentient species. They are visually almost indistinguishable from Humans, save for a pair of delicate, retractable antennaepalps that sprout from the frontal region of their skulls, hidden beneath thick, coarse hair. Due to the heavily polluted and toxic atmosphere of their homeworld, Balosars have evolved a remarkable resistance to toxins and most poisons. Their physiques are generally considered frail, and their average lifespan is notably short, at around 64 standard years.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE
Balosar society is often viewed with distrust by the wider galaxy; they are frequently stereotyped as shifty swindlers and rogues. The planet itself is a polluted, poverty-stricken world of grimy factories and downtrodden people, where tourism is discouraged. The lowest classes work in sweatshops for poor wages. A devastating factor in Balosar culture is the rampant use and production of ixetal cilona (death sticks), a highly addictive narcotic. Because Balosars are immune to the deadly toxic components of the drug, they can consume mass amounts without immediate physical harm—but they are not immune to its life-long addictive properties. Many Balosars come from profound poverty and have had poor medical care, if any at all, throughout their lives.

HISTORY
Located in the Core Worlds, the industrialized planet Balosar was discovered by the Galactic Republic during the Great Manifest Period and joined the Corellian Hegemony during the Alsakan Conflicts. Over time, it became a ruined planet, its night sky blotted out by pollution. During the Old Republic era, the planet remained a poverty-stricken world exploited by corporations, with its people struggling under the weight of environmental devastation and criminal influence.

CULTURAL NOTES

Difficult Patients: Lived experiences tend to leave Balosars cynical, distrustful, and with a limited regard for their own well-being. Getting one to cooperate and communicate necessary medical information is often trying.

Fear of Authority: Patients may withhold information about substance use if they think it will result in legal consequences or if they are involved in criminal activity.

Earning Trust: Maintaining regard for the patient’s autonomy, explaining your actions in detail, and expressing understanding of why they may not trust you will help them feel at ease.

Ignorance of Health: Many may be entirely ignorant of their own medical history or consider chronic diseases to be "normal." Expect to do extended diagnostic work and uncover underlying problems unrelated to the immediate complaint.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Antennaepalps: Located on the frontal region of the head, these are delicate sensory organs. They are sensitive to subsonic frequencies and occasionally sudden surges of emotion from others. Certain medical equipment produces subsonic "noise" that can severely irritate or distress a Balosar patient. Keep the head area clear of heavy machinery.

Liver: While highly resistant to toxins, their livers take immense strain. Liver damage is common; perform blood tests for liver function before administering potentially risky medications.

COMMON DISEASES

Substance Abuse: Rampant among Balosars, usually (but not always) of ixetal cilona.

Environmental Illnesses (Homeworld Born): Individuals born and raised on Balosar may exhibit chronic illnesses from lifelong exposure to heavy pollution, most frequently anemia, respiratory disorders, organ dysfunctions, or autoimmune disorders.

Cancer: Rates among middle-aged and older natives are particularly high due to environmental toxicity.

Mental Health: Depressive disorders and antisocial behaviors are common.

Withdrawal: Habitual cilona users begin showing withdrawal symptoms within eighteen to twenty-four hours of abstention. Symptoms include sweating, sudden agitation, tachycardia, tremor, nausea, sensitivity to light or touch, skin rash, and in severe cases, seizures.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Medication Dosages: Balosars are highly toxin-resistant and will require significantly higher doses of anesthetics, sedatives, and other medications.

Substance Disclosure: It is of the utmost importance, for the patient’s safety, to convince them to disclose any illicit substances they may have recently taken. Ixetal cilona is a processed mycotoxin and may be counteracted accordingly. Prepare to manage abrupt withdrawal.

Misdiagnosis Warning: Do not mistake a Balosar who looks "sickly" as a substance user. Their toxin resistance prevents the organ damage seen in users of other species. A sickly appearance is characteristic of long-term exposure to their homeworld’s toxic environment and should be taken as a risk factor for related diseases.

Extended Care: Prepare for extended diagnostic work. Treat the patient with patience, as their lack of prior medical care means you may be uncovering years of untreated issues.

BOTHAN

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Bothans are furry, bipedal mammalian humanoids from the planet Bothawui. They have expressive, large ears, elongated snouts, and keen eyesight and hearing. Their fur ranges from brown and tan to gray and black, and they have retractable claws on their digits. They are naturally agile and quick, with a heightened sense of smell used for communication and tracking. Bothans live roughly 80 standard years.  

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Bothan society is highly political and clan-based. They value information, cunning, and manipulation above physical strength. Their culture revolves around intricate webs of alliances, favors, and espionage—politics is their primary sport. Merit is earned through successful scheming and intelligence-gathering. They are fiercely proud of their species and notoriously secretive, rarely trusting outsiders with their true intentions.  

HISTORY

Long before the Old Republic formed, Bothans evolved on the savannas of Bothawui as pack-hunting predators. Their early history is marked by constant inter-clan warfare over territory and resources. They developed advanced communication networks using scent and sound long before achieving spaceflight. Their first interstellar contact came via hostile traders, which taught them the value of information as a weapon—leading them to become master spies and negotiators even in their pre-Republic era. By the time the Republic expanded into their sector, Bothan clans had already unified under a loose parliamentary system, forged through centuries of bloody power struggles.  

CULTURAL NOTES

The Bothan Council: The ruling body, made up of clan representatives, but internal rivalries remain fierce.

Reputation Known: across the galaxy as untrustworthy schemers, a label they wear with pride—they see it as proof of their intelligence.

Rites: They practice elaborate funeral rites where the deceased's secrets are ritually recited and passed to their heir.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Ears (Large, mobile): Rich in blood vessels. Ear laceration = major bleed risk.

Lungs (Small): Susceptible to smoke inhalation. Bothans are claustrophobic due to warren origins.

Liver: Hyper-efficient at filtering toxins; poisons work slower, but crash harder.

COMMON DISEASES

Whisper-Lung: Neurodegenerative disease specific to Bothawui's air filters; causes paranoia.

Data-Fever: Psychosomatic illness from information overload (spy burnout).

Snout Ulcers: Stress-induced lesions.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Silence Therapy: Bothans heal faster in absolute quiet (no beeping monitors).

Tranquilizers (Low dose): Their metabolism resists sedatives; but overdose is easy due to small body mass.

Patch over eyes: Reduces visual stimulus to lower cortisol.

Medical personnel must be absolutely honest with a Bothan patient; they will know if you are withholding information. Treat them in quiet, private environments, expect them to read your body language. Respect their boundaries, and they will trust your hands."

CATHAR

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Cathar are bipedal feline humanoids from the planet Cathar . Their bodies are covered in fur with thick manes, and they possess prominent, retractable claws and elongated canine teeth . Male Cathar typically have longer fangs extending from the lower jaw and may have short beards . They stand between 1.5 and 1.9 meters tall . Their bodies have rapid healing abilities, making them formidable hand-to-hand combatants . Two less feline subspecies exist, known as the Juhani and Myr Rho . They are biologically similar to Bothans and are born in litters. 

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Cathar society is clan-based, governed by Elders, and places high value on loyalty, honor, and passion . They live in cities carved into giant trees, with friezes depicting their heroes and history . Cathar mate for life, and a surviving mate will never take another partner. Their culture features grand pageants and a religious ritual called the "Blood Hunt," where warriors fight entire Kiltik nests to gain honor and purge inner darkness . Their native language is Catharese, often emphasized with growls.

HISTORY

Before the Old Republic era, the Cathar were a proud, independent species not aligned with the Republic . Their history is marked by a prior conflict with the Mandalorian Crusaders during the Great Sith War, where they fought alongside Republic forces against Mandalore the Indomitable on Onderon. This victory earned the Mandalorians' lasting enmity. Now, they lived in their tree-cities on Cathar, maintaining their clan traditions and preparing for the inevitable Mandalorian retaliation.

CULTURAL NOTES

Force Sensitivity: Force-sensitive Cathar often become Jedi, though the Jedi way is known to oppose their natural instincts.

Galactic Reputation: Known for their loyalty and ferocity, they are respected as great warriors.

Offspring: Force-sensitive cubs are often sent to the Jedi Temple.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Claws (retractable): Located in fingers and possibly toes. Damage to claw beds could impair natural weaponry and hunting ability. 

Teeth / fangs: Carnivorous predators. Dental health is critical for eating and combat. 

Ears: Feline-like, likely sensitive to high-frequency sounds. Loud environments or ear injuries could be disorienting. 

Tail (if present): Used for balance during fast movement and combat. Tail fractures could affect agility.

COMMON DISEASES

Potential concerns: Fur parasites, feline-specific infections, litter birth complications

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Claw injuries: Retractable claws that do not retract properly may indicate tendon or joint damage. Healing requires restoring mobility without causing pain.

Fur/skin conditions: Burn wounds or infections affecting fur-covered areas need careful cleaning to prevent matting and skin irritation beneath the fur.

Litter birth recovery: Mothers giving birth to multiple offspring (litters) may need extra recovery time and nutritional support.

Bothan-derived treatments: Due to biological similarity, medications or procedures developed for Bothans may be safe for Cathar - but always verify first.

INTERVIEW

Subject: Jedi Knight, Sihiyr Nal

1. I've read that Cathar have a strong instinct to hide pain and illness - that you might not show symptoms even when seriously hurt. If you're injured, would you prefer that I ask you directly about your pain, or should I look for physical signs like decreased appetite, greasy fur, or withdrawal from touch? And is there a way I can help you feel safe enough to tell me the truth about how you're feeling?

Knight Sihiyr explained that this is partly a generalization, since Cathar are viewed as warriors and tend toward stoicism. She suggested that asking directly while also observing physical signs would be most valuable for most Cathar. As for safety, she said it comes down to trust, not unlike with Humans, Togruta, or Zabrak — and that being a Jedi Healer is already enough to establish that trust.

2. Your claws are retractable and very sharp, right? Do you have control over them at all times, or can they extend involuntarily when you're afraid, in pain, or unconscious? If one of your claws breaks or gets injured, does it heal quickly, and does it affect your ability to fight or climb? Can claws be safely trimmed without causing pain or long-term issues?

She confirmed that they are retractable and very sharp, and that they are almost always voluntary — it depends on the individual Cathar's self-control. If one breaks, it's painful and can bleed if broken too far down, but they heal fairly quickly because of good blood flow to that area. Losing one claw is a small disruption, but if all of them broke, it would pose trouble. She didn't specifically mention trimming, but emphasized that control is physiological.

3. Does this rapid healing mean you need different medication doses - or does it mean you might heal wrong if a bone isn't set properly before it starts mending?

She confirmed that Cathar do heal faster, with much less intervention needed. She gave an example of a deep gash on her arm that healed completely — fur and all — in just under a month, with barely a scar. This is supported by a protein-heavy diet, deep rest, and good nutrition. Medications help further. However, she stressed that yes, bones can set incorrectly just like with anyone else if not properly aligned before they start mending.

4. I've heard that some Cathar tribes have very detailed knowledge of medicinal herbs and healing techniques - some of which rival modern kolto treatments. Do you know any of these traditional remedies? Would you prefer herbal treatments over synthetic medicines, or do you trust both equally? And are there any Cathar healing practices that are part of your medical tradition?

She said 'had' would be the better term — since she returned to Cathar and there are no more tribes left. Historically, healing was a communal effort and a matter of convenience, which may have turned it into legend. She personally isn't picky about treatment types, as long as they are ethical — she trusts both herbal and synthetic medicines equally.

5. Are there any medical procedures, body parts, or practices that Cathar culture considers dishonorable or forbidden? For example, would a Cathar refuse blood transfusion, organ donation, or amputation on cultural grounds?  

She mentioned that to her knowledge, there are no such prohibitions — though it may depend on the individual's personal beliefs. It wasn't the case for her City Tree. So things like blood transfusions, organ donations, or amputations wouldn't be refused on cultural grounds, generally speaking.

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

The Chiss are a near-Human species from the planet Csilla, distinguished by their blue skin, glowing red eyes, and dark blue or black hair . They are tall, with angular facial features . Their eyes see slightly into the infrared spectrum, and their hearing and reflexes are sharper than those of baseline Humans . They reach physical maturity quickly and live roughly 80 standard years.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Chiss society is highly structured and governed by the Chiss Ascendancy, an oligarchy controlled by influential families that function like branches of government. They value order, discipline, art, and intellectual advancement. Their culture has a strict taboo against pre-emptive strikes, meaning they almost never attack unless provoked first, but they retaliate with overwhelming force . Force-sensitive females, known as "sky-walkers," are trained as navigators.

HISTORY

The Chiss are native to the frozen world of Csilla, located in the Unknown Regions—a treacherous expanse of space cut off from the wider galaxy by solar storms, black holes, and gravity wells. The Chiss evolved in complete isolation from the Republic and the Sith Empire, developing an advanced civilization known as the Chiss Ascendancy.

For thousands of years, the Ascendancy was ruled by the Nine Ruling Families—an oligarchic aristocracy that governed through a body called the Syndicure. Their society was highly regimented, valuing order, discipline, and the supremacy of family over the individual . The Chiss maintained strict isolationist policies and a prohibition against preemptive strikes, requiring proof of an impending attack before engaging in war.

CULTURAL NOTES

Isolation: They remain largely isolated, with limited contact with the rest of the galaxy.

Xenophobia: They are cautious of outsiders and view their civilization as superior.

Family System: Family ties dominate political and social life, and promising individuals can be adopted into influential families for merit.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Eyes (Luminous red, pupil-less): Sensitive to oxygen concentration changes. Protect from extreme bright light; infrared adaptation may cause discomfort under intense illumination. Do not mistake eye color changes for medical emergency — this is normal physiological response to oxygen levels.

Circulatory System: Comparable to Human baseline. Standard emergency protocols apply.

Nervous System: Comparable to Human baseline. No known anatomical deviations.

Reproductive System: Comparable to Human baseline. Note accelerated maturation rate for pediatric considerations.

COMMON DISEASES

Thermal Stress (Hypothermia Risk): Despite adaptation to Csilla's frigid climate, Chiss possess few physiological adaptations to cold beyond behavioral. Their lower body temperature (28–32°C) means standard Human fever ranges may indicate critical illness.

Off-World Pathogen Susceptibility: Due to the Chiss Ascendancy's isolation in the Unknown Regions and minimal contact with the wider galaxy, Chiss patients may lack immunity to common Republic/Imperial pathogens.

Metabolic Disorders (Rare):  Active metabolism generally prevents obesity, but rapid metabolic shifts during illness require careful monitoring of nutritional intake.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Temperature Management: Maintain medical bay at cooler temperatures (18–22°C / 64–72°F) for Chiss comfort. Standard Human-warm environments may cause overheating due to their dense metabolism and lower baseline temperature.

Fever Assessment: Chiss normal body temperature is 28–32°C. A reading above 35°C (95°F) constitutes fever. Do not use Human reference ranges.

Pediatric Dosing Adjustment: Due to accelerated growth rate, a Chiss child aged 8–12 may have adult body mass but not full organ maturity. Calculate medication dosages by weight AND developmental stage, not chronological age alone.

Blood Transfusion: As Near-Humans with comparable circulatory systems, Chiss may accept Human blood with proper typing. However, due to evolutionary divergence, cross-match thoroughly before transfusion.

Dark Vision Consideration: When examining eyes, use dimmed red light rather than bright white light. Their infrared-adapted vision may find standard examination lights painfully intense.

DEVARONIAN

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Devaronians are humanoids from the planet Devaron, exhibiting extreme sexual dimorphism. Males are bald with prominent cranial horns, while females are hornless and possess full heads of hair. Both sexes have pointed teeth and black, silver-based blood. They possess two livers that constantly cleanse toxins from their system, granting high resistance to poisons. Their bodies are denser than most humanoids, making them heavier than they appear. Their skin is immune to fire and extreme heat. Skin color ranges from red to green, and eye color varies from red to purple and dark brown.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Devaronians live under a matriarchal government on their homeworld. While males are not prohibited from holding positions of power, few desire to do so. The biological dimorphism of the species carries over into their culture: females tend to be more settled and serious-minded, while males long to wander the stars. Devaronian males are extremely dedicated to their families despite their wanderlust, and are known to send the earnings they accumulate abroad back to their families to support them .

The Devaronian homeworld of Devaron is a planet of low mountains, deep valleys, and thousands of rivers. Preservation of the environment is a cultural phenomenon woven into Devaronian society, allowing the planet to retain its natural beauty . Sparsely populated areas are allowed to thrive in their natural state without urbanization or industrialization spreading beyond contained cities or settlements .

HISTORY

Developing hyperspace technology as early as 30,000 BBY, the Devaronian culture developed quickly from hunter-gatherers to an industrial power in a short amount of time. The species developed a divide in gender roles during their tribal stage when males had to travel several days from settlements to hunt for prey while the females maintained the safety and organization of the camp. While the matriarchal government developed and funded space technology, it was the wanderlust of the males which drove the species to the stars and beyond . Exploring the region of space around Devaron, Devaronian explorers made contact with other hyperspace scouts and helped blaze the Corellian Trade Spine.

One of the earliest planetary governments to join the Galactic Republic, sometime between 25,000 BBY and 22,000 BBY, Devaron was one of the farthest points in known space, forming a border with Wild Space for millennia. While the government of Devaron was a representative government that matured to a point where the strict divisions between gender roles was blurred, Devaronian females were historically the most prestigious government officials.

During the Jedi Civil War, the planet was the site of a battle won by the Republic.

CULTURAL NOTES

Matriarchal Society: Devaron is governed by a matriarchal system. Females tend to be settled, serious-minded, and dominant in politics and home life; males are driven by wanderlust and rarely remain on the homeworld. Clinical implication: A female Devaronian patient may be more accustomed to authority and decision-making. A male patient may be restless and reluctant to remain for treatment.

Wanderlust (Males): Male Devaronians are biologically and culturally driven to explore the stars. They often become tramp freighter captains, scouts, or mercenaries, sending earnings home to their families.

Historical Note (Old Republic): Devaronians developed hyperdrive independently around 27,000 BBY, making them one of the first species in the galaxy to achieve interstellar travel. Devaron was one of the earliest planetary governments to join the Galactic Republic.

Reputation: Devaronians are sometimes perceived as "demons" due to their horns and sharp teeth. They are known for being bold, cunning, and well-suited to lives on the fringe.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Dual Livers: The cornerstone of Devaronian physiology. Their two livers constantly cleanse the blood of toxins and carcinogens, granting exceptional resistance to poison. Clinical implication: Standard poison antidotes may be ineffective. However, the livers struggle to eliminate sulfur—long-term stimulant use can cause cumulative damage.

Blood (Silver-based): Appears thick and black when exposed. The unique composition affects how medications and toxins are processed.

Horns (Males): A point of pride, groomed regularly. Believed to be an evolutionary mutation for fending off predatory birds. Clinical implication: Protect from trauma; broken horns may cause significant pain.

COMMON DISEASES

Sulfur Toxicity (Chronic): Long-term use of sulfur as a stimulant overwhelms the livers' ability to eliminate it, causing cumulative organ damage.

Horn Trauma: Males take great pride in their horns; fractures or breaks are both physically and psychologically significant.

Off-World Pathogen Susceptibility: As a species with extensive interstellar travel history, Devaronians are broadly adapted to galactic pathogens, but individual vulnerability varies.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Poison Resistance Consideration: Do not assume standard toxins will affect a Devaronian as expected. Their dual-liver filtration system may neutralize poisons rapidly. Conversely, substances that bypass or overwhelm the livers (like sulfur) require careful monitoring.

Medication Dosing: The silver-based blood and dual-liver system may alter how medications are metabolized. Start conservatively and monitor effects.

Horn Care: If a male Devaronian's horn is damaged, treat with respect and urgency. The horns are culturally significant and physically sensitive.

Temperature/Environment: No specific vulnerabilities noted. Standard humanoid environmental tolerances apply.

DUROS

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

The Duros are a humanoid species native to the planet Duro, located in the Duro sector of the galaxy's Core Worlds. They possess smooth blue-green skin, red eyes with slit pupils, lipless mouths, and long, thin noseless faces . Their olfactory organs are located in tiny slits beneath their eyes. Both males and females are bald, though other sex characteristics make them easily distinguishable. They are taller and leaner than Humans, averaging around six feet in height.

Duros are descended from reptiles and are an egg-laying species. Females lay eggs that hatch into a larval grub stage, which the family unit raises. Unlike similar species, Duros care for their offspring from birth. They possess an almost photographic memory, allowing them to recall and memorize information with exceptional clarity.

A unique defensive mechanism: when Duros experience high amounts of strain or stress, a kind of oil—which has a highly irritating smell and terrible taste—gathers in sacs along their ribs. This is an evolutionary throwback from when Duros were vulnerable to predators on their homeworld.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Duros culture is primarily focused toward expansionism, storytelling, and exploration. It is heavily encouraged for Duros to travel among the stars in search of new experiences and places. They prefer the honorific term "Traveler" regardless of profession, and exclusively refer to other Duros in the galaxy by this title. Other species who honor this title by calling a Duros a "Traveler" are seen as displaying good etiquette.

Duros society is organized around their starship manufacturers as the governing body of the Duros sector, with shareholders making up the voting body for political situations. They practiced a strict form of lending called The Duros Lending Tradition. Instead of debt being tied to the individual who bought the item, the debt was attached to the item itself. The item could be traded and bartered as long as an honest deal was struck to pay off the remaining debt. If a debtor died before the debt was paid, it was inherited by their next of kin. Failure to pay caused severe social stigma—the debtor was seen as unreliable, and the item was repossessed.

The Duros are known as calm, peaceful, and even-tempered, and are considered dependable workers and gifted storytellers . Their language, Durese, is a trade language that can be learned, spoken, and written by other species. It is one of the influences used for Galactic Basic . A ritual phrase within Duros culture is "Let us become spears".

HISTORY

The Duros were one of the first cultures to develop hyperdrive-equipped ships . They achieved interstellar travel before the formation of the Galactic Republic, pioneering some of the galaxy's oldest trade routes and hyperlanes . They are considered among the most experienced space travelers in the galaxy, rivaling the Corellians.

In ancient times, the Duros were enslaved by the Rakata of the Infinite Empire . After the Infinite Empire fell, the Duros—alongside Humans, Herglics, Devaronians, and other species—overthrew their Rakatan masters . They then reverse-engineered Rakatan technology, removing the parts that depended on the Force, and through a series of experiments, invented the modern hyperdrive . This technological breakthrough laid the foundation for subsequent galactic exploration and the establishment of the Galactic Republic.

As a colonial species, the Duros migrated and adapted to dominate many worlds . They largely neglected their homeworld of Duro, which became a polluted, over-industrialized world over time . One of their earliest colonies, Neimoidia, eventually gave rise to the Neimoidian subspecies, which diverged physically by 15,000 BBY . The Duros generally refused to acknowledge the Neimoidians as genetic descendants, despite scientific evidence proving their relationship.

During the Mandalorian Wars, Duro's orbital cities were attacked by Mandalorian warlords in their Basilisk war droids. The planet was believed to have remained conquered for a time until Revan's Jedi retook it.

CULTURAL NOTES

Nomadic Mindset: Duros hate being confined to a medbay; discharge as soon as safe.

Pilots' Pride: They will hide vertigo symptoms because it threatens their piloting identity.

Colony Ship Protocol: Many Duros have no "homeworld" medical records; treat each as unique.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Eyes (Oversized): Pupils dilate poorly; sudden bright light causes temporary blindness and pain.

Liver (Triple-lobed): Filters heavy metals; but susceptible to alcohol poisoning.

Skin (Thin, no subcutaneous fat): Tears easily; sutures must be extra-fine.

COMMON DISEASES

Light Blindness (Photokeratitis): UV damage to eyes from forgetting protective goggles.

Skin Delamination: Skin peels in sheets from dehydration or chemical exposure.

Space Madness (Psychosis): Long-term space-dwelling Duros lose spatial orientation.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Dim Lighting: Treat Duros in low-light rooms; use IR monitors instead of bright screens.

Fine Sutures (5-0 or smaller): Their skin cannot hold thick stitches.

Ammonia-Buffered Fluids: Standard IV fluids cause ammonia toxicity in Duros blood.

ECHANI

 

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

The Echani are a near-Human species with pale skin, white hair, and silver eyes, distinguished by a striking uniformity in body type and facial features—close family members often appear nearly identical to outsiders. The Thyrsian subspecies, however, diverge from this pattern with dark hair and darker skin. The Echani are not biologically precognitive; their reputation for predicting an opponent's movements in combat arises from a culture where martial combat is the primary form of communication, honing their ability to read intent through physical expression rather than any inherent Force ability.  

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

The Echani are a matriarchal, caste-based society originating from the Inner Rim world of Eshan, ruling a confederacy of six worlds known as the Six Sisters through an all-female council known as the Echani Command. Their culture is defined by kinetic communication—the belief that true understanding comes through combat, which they view as a form of self-expression and art rather than mere violence. Echani children often appear nearly identical to outsiders, making the ability to read subtle body language an essential survival skill. They favor light armor and melee weapons, valuing agility and movement over heavy protection, and are renowned as master craftsmen of vibroswords and personal energy shields. Though they have no innate precognitive abilities, their martial tradition allows them to read intent through physical movement with extraordinary precision, a skill that has made them formidable warriors and occasional allies to the Jedi Order.

HISTORY

The Echani are a culture of respected warriors, renowned for producing exceptional duelists. Their long-standing rivalry with the Mandalorians drew them into the Mandalorian Wars on the side of the Republic, with Echani General Yusanis fighting alongside the Jedi Exile Arren Kae until the Battle of Malachor V. Following the assassination of an Echani Senator by Darth Revan during the Jedi Civil War, Yusanis challenged Revan to a duel and was defeated—yet the Echani, like the Mandalorians before them, came to respect Revan's combat skill. Internal tensions later led to the Begali Uprising and the eventual secession of Thyrsus, whose special forces evolved into the Sun Guard. Despite these divisions, Echani martial traditions remained influential, their fighting techniques and weapon designs—including double-bladed vibroblades—persisting through the centuries as a testament to their enduring legacy as warriors.  

CULTURAL NOTES

Communication through Combat: An Echani patient will likely be uncomfortable with purely verbal communication. They read physicality and intent. When treating them, move with purpose and deliberate calm. A sudden or hesitant touch will be interpreted as uncertainty or deception.

Uniformity: Because Echani siblings can look identical, identity verification is crucial in a medical setting. Rely on voice, specific scars, or personal identification rather than visual recognition.

The Six Sisters: The Echani Command rules their space. If treating a female Echani, especially from Eshan or a core world of the Six Sisters, they may hold a position of high authority or caste. Treat them with the respect due to a commander.

No Armor: Echani duels forbid armor. If treating an Echani warrior, expect injuries to be lacerations or blunt trauma, not blaster burns or shrapnel wounds.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Enhanced Nervous System: Their nervous system is significantly faster than a baseline Human's, granting them extreme reflexes and agility. Their reaction time is so fast that they may flinch or move involuntarily during a procedure before the pain signal is consciously processed. Use sedatives or restraints carefully; a startled Echani is dangerous.

Dense Musculature: Their muscle fibers are remarkably dense. Intramuscular injections may require longer needles or higher pressure. Be aware of their high physical strength during recovery.

Standard Humanoid Vital Organs: Their internal anatomy is largely near-Human. However, their cardiovascular system has higher oxygen concentrations in the blood, indicating a highly efficient metabolic engine.

COMMON DISEASES

Overstrain Injuries: Their culture of constant martial training and kinetic communication leads to chronic joint wear, micro-fractures, and tendon strain.

Sensory Overload (Kinetic): In environments with excessive chaotic movement (like a crowded, panicked medbay), an Echani may experience severe anxiety or aggression due to the "noise" of bad body language and intent surrounding them.

Potential concerns: Light sensitivity (ocular), skin conditions related to pale complexion, genetic uniformity may mean less resistance to certain novel pathogens.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Eye care: Silver eyes may require specialized treatment. Protect from extremely bright light. Any vision loss would be devastating for an Echani warrior.

Muscle/nerve healing: Since Echani communicate through subtle body movement, nerve damage that causes tremors or stiffness could be psychologically as well as physically traumatic – they lose their "voice."

Combat-related injuries: Fractures, sprains, contusions from frequent fighting. Prioritize full mobility over simple healing.

Psychological care: An Echani who cannot fight may feel unable to express themselves or connect with others. Healing must include emotional support, not just physical.

Thyrsian subspecies: Darker skin may have different sun sensitivity and healing patterns. Do not assume same dermatological treatments as mainline Echani.

EWOK

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Ewoks are sentient, diminutive, furry bipeds. They are covered in fur from head to toe, most commonly in shades of brown and black, though white, gold, and rare red coats exist. Some individuals even sport stripes. They possess large, bright eyes, small humanoid noses, and hands with two fingers and an opposable thumb. Despite their small stature, Ewoks are physically robust, strong enough to overpower combat-trained Humans, and possess an acute sense of smell. They are omnivorous mammals. Notably, their biology results in a shorter natural lifespan compared to many other species

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Ewok society is tribal and deeply spiritual, structured around villages led by a Chief and a Council of Elders. They are curious, friendly, and social beings, with dance and music playing a major role in their daily lives. Their culture is rich in oral tradition, art, and mythology, including the worship of "Tree Spirits" and the belief in figures like the "Golden One". Each village typically has a medicine man or shaman who is a keeper of mystical lore, herbal remedies, and healing rituals.

CULTURAL NOTES

Medicine Men & Shamans: Ewok healing is deeply tied to spirituality and nature. If an Ewok patient is accompanied by a medicine man, respect their authority. They use curative wands, spirit staffs, and forest-derived medicinal plants.

Technology & Fear: Ewoks are quick learners when exposed to advanced technology with simple mechanical concepts, but a medbay's loud, sterile environment may terrify them. Keep medical droids at a distance and explain every tool before use.

Dietary & Predator Awareness: Ewoks are omnivorous, but they are also prey to larger creatures. Be aware that some species consider Ewoks a food source; do not joke about this, as it is a deep cultural trauma.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Nervous System & Senses: They possess an acute sense of smell and large eyes adapted for the forest canopy. Sudden bright lights or harsh chemical smells will cause severe disorientation or panic.

Musculoskeletal System: Despite their size, they have dense muscle mass. They can easily overpower a Human if panicked. Sedate carefully.

Respiratory System: Adapted to the humid, dense forest air. Dry, recycled starship air may cause respiratory irritation.

COMMON DISEASES

Toxin & Fungal Poisoning: The forests of Endor are home to deadly flora, such as the Rokna tree fungus. Ewoks are highly susceptible to these natural toxins if exposed off-world.

Physical Trauma: Their arboreal lifestyle makes falls from heights (tree villages) a common cause of fractures and spinal trauma.

Respiratory Infections: Off-world environments can easily sicken Ewoks.

Zoonotic Diseases: Living in close proximity to forest creatures means Ewoks are prone to parasites and zoonotic infections.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Herbal Medicine Synergy: Ewok physiology responds well to natural, plant-based remedies. If possible, incorporate herbal analgesics or poultices alongside standard kolto to ease their anxiety and speed healing.

Pediatric Dosing: Due to their small mass, all medications and anesthetics must be dosed at pediatric or reduced levels.

Psychological Comfort: Treat them in a warm, humid environment. Allow them to keep their tribal clothing or weapons if they are not a danger, as stripping them of their gear can induce severe panic.

Fur Inspection: Their dense fur can hide wounds, bruises, or parasites. Always part the fur completely to inspect the underlying skin.

GAND

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Gand are a sentient, insectoid species of stocky build, possessing brown skin, two-toed feet, and three-fingered hands. Their heads feature a pair of large, silver, compound eyes.

The most defining biological trait of the Gand is their respiratory dichotomy. There are two distinct subspecies:

  1. Breathing Gand: This rare subspecies possesses lungs adapted exclusively to breathe ammonia, the primary component of Gand's atmosphere. They require specialized respirators or breath masks to survive in oxygen-rich environments. Oxygen is highly toxic to them, capable of burning the inside of their lungs like acid. However, their lungs are remarkably efficient, able to utilize nearly 85% of the atmosphere with each breath
  2. Lungless Gand: This subspecies does not breathe at all. They draw sustenance entirely through the absorption of food intake and approximate speech by modulating sounds (often described as flatulence).

Genetically, Gand are distantly related to the Kel Dor and Tognath species, who share similar respiratory requirements for off-world travel.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Gand society is defined by profound humility. Their culture dictates that an individual's identity must be earned. Young Gand have no names, and as they accomplish deeds, they earn basic names and eventually surnames.

Because identity is earned, most Gand are self-deprecating and polite, referring to themselves in the third person. The use of first-person pronouns is reserved only for the most legendary Gand, as it presumes one is so renowned that everyone already knows their name. A Gand who has failed or feels deep shame will refer to themselves simply as "Gand."

HISTORY

Gand is a gaseous planet shrouded in thick, ammonia-based mists, with pocket colonies ruled by a long-standing totalitarian government. The planet was discovered by the wider galaxy prior to 3951 BBY. 

Nowadays, the planet remain independent of the Galactic Republic. 

Leaving the homeworld is traditionally against Gand custom, but some ventured into the stars when the old traditions began to fade or when duty called.

CULTURAL NOTES

The Third Person: Do not mistake their speech pattern for confusion or a translation error. Referring to themselves in the third person is a sign of humility. Only address them by a first-person name if they have explicitly earned a legendary status.

Earned Names: A Gand's name reflects their deeds. If a Gand patient introduces themselves simply as "Gand," they are expressing deep shame or humility. Treat them with respect and do not pry into their past failures.

The Findsmen: Some Gand belong to a mystic tradition of trackers called Findsmen. They are Force-sensitive, though they attribute their abilities to reading omens in the mists of Gand. If a Gand patient mentions being a Findsman, they may have sensory abilities beyond the norm.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Respiratory System (Breathing Gand): Highly efficient but exclusively adapted to ammonia. The respirator is a literal lifeline. If a breathing Gand is brought into a medbay without their mask, or if the mask is damaged, they are suffocating and their lungs are burning. Immediate isolation and administration of an ammonia mixture are required.

Gas Bladder (Lungless Gand): Used for speech approximation. Protect from abdominal trauma.

Compound Eyes: Large and silver. Adapted to the fog of their homeworld. May be sensitive to sudden bright, direct light.

COMMON DISEASES

Oxygen Toxicity: Accidental exposure to oxygen-rich atmospheres for Breathing Gand causes severe internal burns to the lungs.

Respirator Malfunction: Mechanical failure of their life-support gear, leading to rapid asphyxiation.

Atmospheric Shock: Lungless Gand can survive in almost any atmosphere, but sudden pressure changes can cause internal distress.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Respirator Protocol: Never remove a Breathing Gand's mask unless they are in a sealed, ammonia-rich environment or you are actively replacing it with a new life-support system.

Surgical Environment: If a Breathing Gand requires surgery, the operating theater must be filled with their required ammonia atmosphere, or they must remain attached to a closed-circuit respirator.

Medication Dosing: Very little is known about Gand pharmacology. Start with conservative doses and monitor closely, as their insectoid physiology and dual subspecies (lungs vs. lungless) may process chemicals differently.

Sedation: Lungless Gand absorb nutrients (and likely drugs) through ingestion. Breathing Gand require gas-based or intravenous anesthetics.

MIRALUKA

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Miraluka are a Near-Human, Force-sensitive species. They are visually indistinguishable from Humans in every way except for their faces, which completely lack eyes. In their place, they possess vestigial eye sockets covered by skin. 

Because Alpheridies orbits a red dwarf star emitting only infrared light, the Miraluka evolved to perceive their surroundings entirely through Force Sight. This ability allows them to see the Force radiation emitted by all living and non-living things, granting them a form of 360-degree spatial awareness. Their brains evolved to compensate for the loss of eyes: their hearing is highly enhanced, and their hands and feet are extremely sensitive to external stimuli.

Despite their physiological closeness to Humans, mating between the two species often leads to unpredictable and severe genetic complications for the offspring. Miraluka pregnancies last approximately eleven standard months.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Miraluka society is deeply spiritual and built entirely around their connection to the Force. They view their Force Sight not as a disability, but as a profound gift that allows them to perceive the universe on a deeper level than species reliant on physical light.

Out of modesty and cultural tradition, Miraluka almost always cover their eyeless faces with headbands, masks, or decorated veils. Because they are naturally Force-sensitive, many Miraluka are discovered by the Jedi Order at a young age. They make exceptional Jedi, particularly as Consulars or Seers. They tend to be peaceful, philosophical, and deeply empathetic, having spent their lives sensing the emotional and physical energies of the world around them.

HISTORY

The Miraluka originally hailed from a different world, but migrated to Alpheridies thousands of years ago after their original homeworld became uninhabitable.

Nowadays, the Miraluka established a thriving colony on the planet Katarr. However, shortly after the Jedi Civil War, Katarr hosted a secret Jedi convocation. The Sith Lord Darth Nihilus, a wound in the Force who consumed all life, devoured the entire planet. Every living thing on Katarr was stripped of life. The only survivor was a Miraluka Jedi named Visas Marr, who was spared to serve as Nihilus’s apprentice before eventually being redeemed by the Jedi Exile, Meetra Surik.

During the subsequent Great War between the Republic and the Sith Empire, the Alpheridies system remained independent, but many Miraluka rallied to the Republic's cause, fighting fiercely against the Sith.

CULTURAL NOTES

Respect the Veil: Miraluka cover their eyeless faces. Do not ask them to remove their mask or headband unless medically necessary for a procedure.

Not Disabled: Never treat a Miraluka patient as if they are disabled. In a galaxy brimming with the Force, their sight is often superior to physical vision. They can sense things a baseline Human cannot.

Force-Null Anxiety: Miraluka are deeply unsettled by things that do not exist in the Force—such as droids, ysalamiri, or Force-null zones. Being near such things feels to them like a deafening, blinding void. Keep medical droids at a distance or explain their presence clearly.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Vestigial Eye Sockets: Though covered by skin, these areas are highly sensitive to extreme temperatures (both intense heat and frigid cold). Protect the face during environmental exposures.

The Force Connection (Primary Sense): Their brain chemistry relies entirely on the Force to process spatial data. If a Miraluka is placed in a Force-null zone (like a ysalamiri bubble) or subjected to Force suppression, they will be rendered completely, terrifyingly blind and severely disoriented.

Enhanced Nervous System: Their skin and hearing are highly sensitive. Use gentle touch and speak in calm, moderate tones. Avoid loud, sudden noises or harsh chemical irritants.

COMMON DISEASES

Force Deprivation Psychosis: Severe psychological distress, panic attacks, and disorientation caused by prolonged exposure to a Force-null environment.

UV-Related Skin Damage: Because their homeworld only receives infrared light, Miraluka have no natural resistance to ultraviolet radiation. When traveling off-world, they have a much higher incidence of skin cancer and severe sunburns compared to other species.

Hybrid Genetic Complications: Rare, but if treating a patient of mixed Human/Miraluka heritage, expect unexpected physiological anomalies.

Sensory Overload: In highly populated areas or chaotic battlefields, sensing the intense emotions and Force signatures of thousands of beings can cause migraines or sensory burnout.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Verbalize Everything: Because they cannot see standard medical readouts, charts, or gestures, you must verbally explain every procedure, what you are holding, and what they will feel.

Screen for UV Damage: Always conduct full-body skin checks on off-world Miraluka patients to check for melanoma or lesions.

Force Healing: Miraluka respond exceptionally well to Force-based healing (Curato Salva) because their bodies are already deeply attuned to the Force.

Anesthesia Considerations: Due to their unique brain chemistry, standard sedatives may have unpredictable effects. Monitor their Force signature for signs of distress when putting them under.

Provide a Force Anchor: If a Miraluka patient is panicking or disoriented, grounding them with a strong, calm Force presence (or holding their hand) can help them re-center.

INTERVIEW

ELUNAS'S NOTE

"The Miraluka are a profound reminder that sight is not limited to the eyes. To watch a Miraluka navigate a room is to watch someone dancing with the universe. However, their biology is a delicate balance. They are incredibly resilient in the Force, yet physically fragile to the mundane dangers of the galaxy, like a harsh sun or a Force-nulling beast. A healer must remember to be their eyes when they need it, to speak the physical world into existence for them, and to guard their skin from the light they cannot see. Treat them with the respect due to a species that sees the galaxy not as it looks, but as it truly is."

ZABRAK

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Zabrak are a near-Human species evolved on the harsh, brutal world of Iridonia. They are distinguished by a crown of vestigial horns that develop at puberty. Hair tends to grow in accordance with horn placement; notably, Zabraks lack eyebrows, eyelashes, and facial hair. Their skin tones range from pale to dark brown, with red and orange common on certain colonies. Their bodies are often covered in ritual tattoos.

Internally, Zabraks are mesocarnivores (requiring a diet of 50-70% meat, supplemented by carbs/starches) and possess two hearts. This dual-heart system circulates highly oxygenated blood at a faster rate than most humanoids, granting enhanced endurance, speed, and increased pain resistance. Because of this rapid circulation, Zabraks bleed more heavily when cut and process intravenously administered medications much faster. They are also believed to have a heightened sense of smell, typical of predator species.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE
Zabrak society is defined by self-assurance, fierce independence, and pride. Their language has a distinct baritone quality. They were one of the earliest spacefaring species, establishing eight colonies in five systems before encountering the Republic.

Zabrak culture is deeply tied to nature and survival. They practice elaborate dances for war, courtship, and elemental movements (earth, wind, water, fire), likely used to connect with and calm Iridonia's harsh environment. Clans feature roles beyond warriors, including shamans (often Force-sensitives) and a reverence for a "Goddess" tied to nature or the Force. Younglings are required to learn a native martial art. Tattoos are deeply personal, representing lineage, rites of adulthood, challenges, or personality. The tattooing process can result in accidental scarification, and refusing to undergo these rites is considered shameful.

HISTORY

Iridonia is an arid world of acid pools, seas, and deep, windy canyons. Its government operates as a dictatorship led by a council of the strongest and most proven leaders from the major clans, reflecting a "survival of the fittest" philosophy.

Nowadays, the Zabrak face immense turmoil. Iridonia was attacked and occupied by the Mandalorians during the Mandalorian Wars for quite some time before it was liberated. Later, during the Jedi Civil War, the planet was occupied by the Sith.

CULTURAL NOTES
Titles and Honorifics: Respect their social structure. Prefixes are used to denote status: am' (Youngling/Initiate), vy' (Padawan), ay' (Wise), va' (Proven), and ay'va' (Proven and Wise/Elder). A Knight may be addressed as ay'vy'.

Tattoos & Scarification: Tattoos are sacred rites of adulthood or challenges. Do not touch them without permission. Note that their tattoos often cause intentional or accidental scarification; do not mistake these scars for fresh wounds or abuse.

Pain and Pride: They possess high pain tolerance and a culture that prizes survival. They may underreport pain.

Independence: They are stubborn and self-reliant. Frame medical care as a challenge to overcome together, rather than a directive.

CRITICAL ORGANS
Dual Hearts: The two hearts must work in perfect synchronization. Trauma to one heart can cause the other to fibrillate. Monitor both rhythms continuously during chest trauma.

Cardiovascular System: Rapid blood circulation. Expect severe, rapid blood loss from lacerations. Apply pressure and cauterize immediately.

Cranial Horns: Sensitive vestigial structures. Damage is highly painful. Treat with care.

COMMON DISEASES

Rapid Exsanguination: Due to their fast circulation, they are at high risk of bleeding out from injuries that would be minor for a Human.

Zabrak Scalp Necrosis: A low-grade flesh-eating virus that causes cranial horns to fall out. A vaccine exists.

Overconfidence Injuries: Reckless behavior stemming from their cultural belief that nothing is impossible.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Hemostatic Protocols: Prioritize stopping blood loss immediately.

IV Medication Adjustments: Because they process IV medications rapidly, standard doses may wear off quickly. Monitor closely; do not overcorrect and cause toxicity.

Dietary Needs: Provide a high-protein, meat-heavy diet during recovery, supplemented with carbs.

Pain Management: Do not rely on verbal reports of pain. Monitor vitals. Be aware they may require higher doses of sedatives due to their high tolerance.

Healing Acceleration: Some Zabraks possess organ regeneration, halving healing times.

ZELTRON

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE
Zeltrons are a near-Human species, distinguished by their skin, which ranges in shades of red from light pink to deep crimson, and their hair, typically red or blue. They are widely considered highly attractive. They possess an incredible metabolic rate, allowing them to eat richly and remain in excellent physical shape.

Biological adaptations include a secondary liver and a comparatively denser central and peripheral nervous system. It is speculated by xenobiologists that their distinctive coloration and secondary liver are natural evolutionary adaptations to the unique contaminants or radiation present in Zeltros's Type I atmosphere.

Zeltrons possess two famous physiological traits—though the magnitude varies greatly by individual genetics:

  1. Potent Pheromones: Said to have a calming effect and influence amicability.
  2. Subconscious Empathy: The ability to receive emotions, and in rare cases, project them onto others.

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Zeltron society is communal, with a great fascination for romance, entertainment, and recreational pursuits. Large, long celebrations are commonplace, and hedonism is the encouraged doctrine. The availability and selection of recreational intoxicants are commensurately high, both natively and through interstellar trade, and Zeltrons possess a notable tolerance for alcohol. Because of their empathic abilities, positive emotions are of utmost importance, while negative emotions are actively shunned.

While many species view them foremost as hedonistic, Zeltrons encountered away from their homeworld trend more cosmopolitan. They are highly gifted with holograms (creating Hologram Fun World) and favor wildly colorful or revealing attire.

HISTORY

Zeltros is an opulent world in the Inner Rim where hedonism is the encouraged doctrine, existing in a state of never-ending revelry. The Zeltron appreciation of beauty led to luxurious art and architecture, making the planet a favored vacation destination for the galaxy's wealthy. The planet was ruled by an elective monarchy and joined the Galactic Republic by 4000 BBY. Despite its leisure-focused atmosphere, Zeltros maintained a substantial defense force, though it lacked planetary shielding.

CULTURAL NOTES

Hedonism and Pleasure: Zeltros is a world of endless revelry. Do not judge a Zeltron for their pursuit of pleasure; it is central to their biology and culture.

Aversion to Negativity: They actively shun anger, fear, and depression. A Zeltron may downplay their own pain or illness to avoid bringing negativity into a room.

Monogamy: They view monogamy as impractical. Do not assume a Zeltron patient has a single "next of kin" in the traditional sense.

Familiarity: Many spacers remark that a Zeltron seems extremely familiar, even if they have never met. This is a side effect of their pheromones.

CRITICAL ORGANS

Dual Livers: Zeltrons possess a secondary liver. Clinical Implication: A liver biopsy should be performed on both organs, as their condition may differ. Zeltrons can exhibit a good quality of life with only a single liver, but lifestyle changes must be considered to account for reduced blood filtering and infection resistance.

Dense Nervous System: Their denser central and peripheral nervous systems make them highly receptive to stimuli. Clinical Implication: Extra care should be taken when evaluating anesthesia or stimulant dosages.

Pheromone Glands & Empathic Receptors: Clinical Implication: Their pheromones may cause false familiarity in the healer. Their subconscious empathy means they will sense your true emotional state. Maintain professional detachment and project calm.

COMMON DISEASES

Psychological Risks: Predisposition to thrill-seeking behavior, overindulgence, and chemical dependencies (recreational or medicinal stimulants).

Empathic Trauma: Depression or psychotic episodes resulting from absorbing peer trauma.

Misdiagnosis Risks: Their emotional expressions can sometimes be misdiagnosed as mania or lacking emotional regulation when compared to baseline near-humans.

Cardiovascular Health: They actually have low instances of elevated blood pressure and cardiovascular disease due to their high metabolic rate.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Substance & Toxin Protocol: Always ask about allergens, medical history, and clarify potential recreational intoxicants. Avoid poisons and toxins on the near-human hazard list, but remember their alcohol tolerance is notably high.

Vital Sign Monitoring: Erythema (redness) or facial flushing can be hard to notice on red/pink skin. Routinely check blood flow or pulse readings instead of relying on visual color changes.

Nutritional Care: Their increased caloric burn requires additional sustenance during bedrest or inactivity. Watch for anemia, weight loss, and muscle atrophy.

Mental Health: Therapy and a healthy support network improve health outcomes for Zeltrons beyond that expected for most near-humans.

Modesty Protocol: Zeltrons anecdotally display reduced inhibitions around public nudity. It may be necessary to explicitly highlight the use of any provided modesty gowns or dressing rooms.

Surgical Precautions: While stereotypes and mistrust around Zeltrons are generally unnecessary, in rare cases, additional controls around visitations and staff selection should be considered during awake surgery (due to pheromone/empathic interference).
