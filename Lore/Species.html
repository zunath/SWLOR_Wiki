ARKANIAN

BIOLOGY AND APPEARANCE

Arkanians are a near-Human species from the frigid world of Arkania . They are bipedal humanoids with pale skin tones, white hair, and stark white eyes that lack a visible iris or pupil . A key feature is their hands, which possess four clawed digits: three fingers and a thumb . Their evolution on a dark, cold planet grants them the ability to see into the infrared spectrum, which causes light sensitivity off-world . They frequently enhance their bodies with cybernetics and genetic manipulation to improve themselves .  

SOCIETY AND CULTURE

Arkanian society operates under a rigid caste system that values pure bloodlines and sharp, logical thinking . Their culture is defined by a deep-seated arrogance; they consider themselves superior to all other species and the pinnacle of evolution . Knowledge and research are highly prized, making them skilled scientists and academics, but they are also willing to profit from their advancements, often selling medical treatments or even biological weapons  

HISTORY

Arkanian history is complex, with their homeworld of Arkania having aligned with both the Republic and the Sith Empire at different times . By the time of the Mandalorian Wars, their long history of genetic experimentation had split the species into many sub-species . One of their most infamous acts was the creation of the Quermian species from the Xexto around 17,000 BBY . Their society later faced the Arkanian Revolution around 50 BBY, a conflict over their genetic experiments on the Yaka species. 

CULTURAL NOTES

Arkanian Offshoots: Genetically engineered subspecies, created to serve specific tasks for their pure-blooded creators . They are often seen as inferior, tools, or slaves, and are not considered "true" Arkanians.

Galactic Reputation: Known galaxy-wide for their arrogance and unsettling genetic research, which has horrified many other species .

CRITICAL ORGANS

Eyes (Pale white, infrared vision):  Highly sensitive to bright light and heat. Protect from sudden intense illumination or extreme heat sources. May require protective lenses or blinders in non-Arkanian environments. Do not shine bright examination lights directly into their eyes.

Clawed Digits (Hands):  Four clawed digits on each hand. Protect from damage during restraint or examination. Claws may be sharp.

Brain:  Arkanians have exceptional cognitive abilities; even the slowest can memorize entire libraries. Monitor for signs of cognitive impairment or head injury as you would for any near-Human.

COMMON DISEASES

Genetic Instability / Degeneration:  Due to millennia of genetic manipulation and the creation of "offshoot" sub-species, some Arkanians may suffer from unforeseen genetic disorders or complications.

Off-World Visual Distress (Photophobia):  Their infrared-adapted eyes are sensitive to the brighter and hotter suns of other worlds, which can cause pain, disorientation, and even temporary blindness.

Cyberware Rejection:  As it is common for Arkanians to enhance themselves with cybernetics, they may suffer from implant rejection or infection.

Sensory Overload:  Their exceptional mental capacity and sensitivity to infrared can lead to sensory overload in high-stimulus environments.

SPECIFIC TREATMENTS

Visual Sensitivity Protocol: When examining an Arkanian, use dimmed or red-shifted lighting in the medical bay. Avoid bright surgical lights directly on the face. If the patient wears blinders, only remove them with explicit permission and in a controlled, low-light environment.

Genetic Assessment:  Before any surgery or major medical treatment, consider a thorough genetic screening. Their unique biology may react differently to standard medications or require specific dosages.

Cyberware Management:  Assess any cybernetic implants for signs of rejection, infection, or malfunction. Standard mechanical and medical maintenance protocols apply.

Mental Health Support:  Arkanians place great value on knowledge and intellect. They may not be forthcoming about cognitive decline or mental health struggles due to their culture's emphasis on perfection.
