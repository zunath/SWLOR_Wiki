November 3, 2026 Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | October 31 Release Notes

This update adds a Mon Cala to Smuggler's Moon starlane, a casino slot machine, Smuggler's Moon housing and a new Republic base wing, gives new characters a Language Token, lets Unarmed abilities work with any weapon, makes one Power Glove equal to two Katars, and refreshes shield, pistol and armor appearances. It also fixes hotbar overlap, Unarmed training, spawn placement, area music and a login problem for some characters.

World and Travel

  • Smuggler's Moon now has a housing area, and the Hyper Dive Cantina has a jukebox.
  • Mon Cala and Smuggler's Moon are connected by a starlane. Speak to Nera in the Elite Hotel on Mon Cala, destroy ten Krakenborne ships in Mon Cala orbit, and return for a Starlane Pass. The beacon at the south edge of Mon Cala orbit leads to Smuggler's Moon, and the return beacon is on the east edge of its orbit.
  • The Smuggler's Moon Casino has a slot machine. Each spin costs 100 credits and payouts reach up to 10,000.
  • The hangar entrance outside the Republic base on Viscara now leads to a new hangar and a new base wing with its own residents and shops. A portal in the new hangar leads to the original hangar, and the front door still leads to the original base.
  • Dock fighters on Smuggler's Moon now stand in the Shipping District cargo yard.

Currencies

  • New characters start with one Language Token. Press Use on the Language Tokens row in the Currencies window to pick any language below rank 20 and raise it to rank 20. Existing characters do not receive one.

Skills

  • Perks that trigger after you apply a control effect, such as Katar's Scrapper Control and Guard Training and Staff's Charged Blows and Skull Rattle, now count statuses applied conditionally, such as Guard Counter III's Daze after a guarded hit and the Daze from Worldbreaker and Reprisal. Breaker Reversal's bonus now applies only to control abilities.
  • Redirecting Guard now matches its description: when you control an enemy that was attacking a party member, that enemy is Challenged for 30 seconds (+15% Enmity toward you). It no longer raises your Enmity with every enemy after any control effect.
  • While dual wielding, the character sheet's Attack and Critical Rate tooltips include the off-hand weapon's values, and the Atk Delay tooltip shows each hand's weapon Delay and the time per attack.
  • Unarmed abilities (Jab, Palm Strike, Finishing Combination, Perfect Form, Dirty Jab, Uppercut, Low Kick and Haymaker) now work with any weapon, like every other combat skill's abilities. The Unarmed mastery trials count techniques landed with any weapon.
  • Unarmed abilities strike with both fists: with Power Gloves or two Katars they add the DMG of both hands. Other weapons add one weapon's DMG.
  • A Power Glove's bonuses now count once for each hand, so one Power Glove grants exactly what two identical Katars do. For example, a glove with Attack +5 gives +10 Attack. Characters already wearing a Power Glove receive the missing bonuses automatically, and examining a Power Glove explains how its bonuses count.
  • Assault Gadgets and Grenadier abilities now cost half as much Stamina (STM), rounded up. The capstones Thermal Detonator and Overload Barrage keep their cost.
  • Bare-handed attacks now train Unarmed instead of Katar, including punches with no gloves or bracer equipped.

Appearances and Text

  • Shield appearances have been replaced with cleaner industrial designs. Your items keep their selected appearance.
  • The Appearance Editor now shows pictures for more body parts and armor pieces that were missing them.
  • The Appearance Editor now names every head, armor, body, helmet, cloak, shield and weapon part instead of showing only its number. The number still appears in the corner of each picture, and the Part # box still takes it.
  • Pelvis style 35 has been merged into a corrected style 34 for both genders. Existing items keep their colors.
  • New /helm and /cloak chat commands show or hide your worn helmet or cloak. They flip the same setting as the Show Helmet and Show Cloak checkboxes in the Appearance Editor.
  • Pistol grips have been refit so pistols sit correctly in the hand.
  • Skirt armor motion and thigh clearance have been adjusted.
  • Dyed metal on Republic Trooper and Havoc armor shows its color correctly again, and the same fix was applied across playable armor and headgear.
  • Robe appearance 236 returns to its original models.
  • Power Gloves now fit correctly over armored hands instead of appearing oversized, and both arms swing while you walk or run.
  • The new staff parts no longer pinch where parts join.

Fixes

  • Capstone dungeons now have a single faction each. Adept enemies spawn nearer the entrance and Specialists deeper in, and inner circle guards stand further apart, deeper in the dungeon, in open floor. Journals and quest givers point to the new locations.
  • The skill lines of each capstone dungeon now hunt the same enemies for each tier. An Adept, Specialist or inner circle kill counts for your line only after you use your line's technique or effect on that enemy, as your journal describes. Kills you already made are kept, and upgraded beast techniques also count.
  • Enemies keep fighting when one party member leaves their leash, such as by respawning at a medical center, instead of resetting to full health while another player is still fighting them.
  • Twin Pistols no longer add 2 lb of phantom weight each time you equip them.
  • Every bench seat in the passenger shuttle can be sat in again.
  • The Complete Job button in the incubator works again for finished jobs after you reopen it.
  • Walk mode now stays on when you log back in, so you no longer have to toggle /walk twice.
  • The Great Arkanian Dragon encounter rewards have received a further balance adjustment.
  • Fixed a server crash that could be triggered by invalid text sent from a game client.
  • Extra hotbar ability slots now show the recharge sweep while an ability is on cooldown.
  • Smithery Control, Craftsmanship and CP Bonus that had been wrongly reduced by an old equipment bug are rebuilt from your worn gear at login and after every equipment change, along with your other gear-based stats.
  • Extra hotbars can no longer overlap each other. A bar dragged, turned on or resized onto another moves to the nearest clear spot, so hovering and right-clicking always work.
  • Random creature and resource spawns no longer appear inside buildings and other structures, such as the burnt barn in Dantooine Ruined Farmlands where Thunes could trap themselves. Spawns also stay clear of the map edges instead of standing half outside the walkable area.
  • Area music keeps playing through dawn and dusk in seventeen areas that went silent, including the Hyper Dive Cantina on Smuggler's Moon.
  • The cantina booth consoles in the Czerka Weapons Testing Facility are now decoration and no longer open a training dialog when clicked.
  • The shuttle status console no longer sits half inside the boarding bay wall.
  • Characters whose file names contain accented letters are now upgraded correctly on the server, so they are no longer refused at login.
