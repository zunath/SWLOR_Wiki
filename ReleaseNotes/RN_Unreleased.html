Upcoming Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | November 3 Release Notes

This update opens the new planet Eshan, gives every crafting and gathering activity its own minigame, adds two perk lines for each crafting skill, rebuilds the Incubator, adds a Stablemaster quest line for more stable slots, and fixes the Republic base, the casino and several other problems.

Eshan

  • Eshan, a cold, snowy world, is now open. Travel there from the galaxy map; its orbit has its own space area and its starport connects to the rest of the planet.
  • Explore Eshan City and its districts (Silver Gate, Highcrest Quarter, Hearth Ward, Verdant Crown, Riverway and the Old Quarter), plus the Keshan Heights and Peaks, Silverwood Expanse, Winter Marches, Veylan Groves, Shimmerdeep, Farmlands, Battlegrounds, Grand Proving Grounds, Scraplands, Shattervein, and the tunnels and caves beneath. A housing area and a spa are included, and Eshan has its own weather.
  • Eshan has its own enemies, a yeti boss, high-level resource spawns, and map pieces that drop from its creatures and rare enemies.
  • New quests include a series of hunting and security jobs (such as Break the Frost Pack, Thyrsian Incursion and Shatter Field Command), a Scraplands smuggler quest, and daily supply quests from Eshan City residents.
  • Two new tameable beasts live on Eshan: the Shattervein Wolf and the Echaolm.
  • New Echani cooking recipes and cold-weather field gear recipes can be learned and crafted.
  • Each Eshan area has an exploration achievement.

Crafting Minigames

  • Each crafting skill has its own minigame in place of the old shared crafting window, and each has a Help page with exact action previews: the Plasma Forge (Smithery), Circuit (Engineering), Fabricator (Fabrication), Kitchen (Agriculture cooking) and Reaction (Espionage).
  • Quick Craft makes up to 10 of a recipe at once, at recipe level + 5 or lower, once you have crafted it successfully by hand.
  • Enhancements transfer to a crafted piece by quality threshold: 50%, 85% and 100%. Food duration scales with quality. A broken piece keeps the 65% loss roll and pays 15% XP.
  • Recipes above level 25 or 30 in the Plasma Forge, Circuit and Fabricator are harder, tuned against fully modded top-tier gear, so gear matters more. Fabrication Control now raises Research Test chance and Craftsmanship adds trials. The Researcher and Chef perk lines are reduced so perks no longer replace gear.
  • Guild hand-ins now give skill XP.
  • Crafting stations in the Veles shops, the Korriban landing, the Jedi Temple and the Mandalorian base are placed so you can use them.
  • New station models and crafting animations are included: forge, spy bench, galley, refinery, research terminal, engineering bench and fabricator, and crafting animations for robes.

Gathering and Research

  • Harvesting has a Prospecting minigame, with Quick Harvest at the old pace.
  • Scavenging has a Search board, with Quick Search under the old rules.
  • Fishing has a Line fight, with Quick Reel that lands fish at the old pace. A landed fish plays a landing animation, and Line moves resolve at once with no wait between them.
  • Each research level now starts with a short Experiment.
  • Gathering rank milestones and tool tier now decide yields. The Might, Perception and Willpower bonuses to harvest bonus items, scavenge attempts and fishing catches no longer apply, so characters who invested in those attributes for gathering lose that bonus.

Perks and Progression

  • Every crafting skill (Engineering, Fabrication, Smithery, Agriculture and Gathering) gains two perk lines, each with a style at rank 20 and a capstone at rank 50.
  • Harvesting, Refining, Refinery Management, Scavenging, Hard Look, Research, Research Projects, Scientific Networking and Droid Assembly are now unlocked by skill rank instead of perks. The SP you spent on them is refunded.
  • Treasure Hunter and Creditfinder move to the General category and require Armor. Their SP is refunded so you can buy them again under the new requirements.
  • Every character receives one optional rebuild token with this update. You are not forced to rebuild.

Beasts

  • The Incubator window is rebuilt. It shows the job stage, each enzyme's color and stats, and purity bars with this stage's gain. It has an Allow mutation checkbox, lists the recipes you know from your field notes with each requirement checked against the job, and shows the outcome odds before you complete a job.
  • A job with a 0% mutation chance no longer mutates 1 time in 1000, and removing an enzyme no longer misreads its color as a stat. Weekday requirements say they use server time (UTC).
  • Every field note can now be found. Notes that could not be obtained before drop from rare elites on the beast's home planet.
  • Failure beasts such as Mustardlash Slime, Ochre Maw and House Cat no longer appear by chance from other recipes. Each has its own weekday recipe.
  • Dorra Vesk at the Anchorhead stables offers the quests Room in the Pens and The Long Haul. Each grants one more stable slot, up to 8 with Stabling 5.
  • Incubation enzymes are now 1x1 items with a new icon for each color. Enzymes you already carry or have stored convert automatically.

World and Travel

  • The Republic base front door on Viscara opens into the Headquarters again. The original entrance hall stays reachable with a base key through the original hangar.
  • Walls in the new Republic base wing no longer let you walk through them or draw as black void.
  • Every slot machine in the Smuggler's Moon Casino can be used, not just one.
  • The Droid Foundry Core and Assembly Line keep their lighting through the day and night cycle instead of going black at night.
  • Market listings are refused for lockboxes with a failed slicing attempt.
  • The market remembers your filters as saved exclusions and has a Select All button.
  • Property entry now shows the property's description, and there is a new Area Description window for setting it.
  • Player-given structure names now show on the placeable.
  • The Accessories Shop sells nine blank props for 50 credits each, which cannot be listed on the market.

Appearances and Text

  • Female Cathar pelvis 14, neck 4 and forearms 6 now match their textures and models.
  • Power Gloves no longer appear oversized on the natural hand styles that were missing a glove fit, including Wookiee hands.
  • Neck 289 and 291 and shoulder 260 capes now hang like the long coats instead of following the arms, and the flared hem of male belt 19 sits closer to the legs.
  • Havoc armor's Metal 1 and Metal 2 dyes now use matching shades, so Metal 2 no longer looks much darker than Metal 1 with the same color.
  • Dyed armor and clothing parts no longer glitter at a distance.
  • Neck 012's respirator now shows its intended art on every playable body instead of an unrelated skin texture on some.
  • The Veil Hood (helmet 053) texture is sharper.
  • About 1,200 gameplay icons that were drawn upside down are now upright.
  • The blank vibroblade top and the legacy full-blade middle parts are available again in the Appearance Editor, so weapons that use them can be edited.
  • Descriptions of First Aid and Force heals and temporary HP now say that Willpower multiplies the amount, from 0.625x at 10 Willpower or less up to 1.25x at 26, instead of listing "plus WIL scaling". Healing itself is unchanged.
  • Neck 152's glasses now take dyes, with separate frame, lens and collar colors.
  • The Appearance Editor no longer offers armor part styles that draw nothing: styles 1 and 2 where None is offered, and shoulder and belt styles that show nothing. Armor already using them keeps working.

Fixes

  • Tier 4 and tier 5 slicing boards are easier, with more room for mistakes, and their rewards are improved: successes pay out fewer tools and more named items, schematics and field notes, and grant more supplies.
  • The Performance window has a Roleplay mode that drops the cues and Flourish and keeps the show going for 10 minutes after you last talk or emote. The cue windows now show what your audience gets.
  • Mobile Footwork, Snap Roll and Kiting Instinct now trigger from any hostile ranged weapon ability (Pistol, Twin Pistol, Rifle or Throwing), matching their descriptions. Ranged Force and Mimicry attacks still do not count.
  • Sniper Stance now adds its +15% damage to hostile ranged weapon abilities used from long range (10 meters or more), instead of being only a penalty.
  • Ordnance Stance now reduces single-target thrown damage by 10% as described, and Flurry Stance now grants its +10% Haste only on thrown attacks against bleeding targets instead of on every attack.
  • Holding a movement key right after a jump or teleport, such as Shadow Step or Force Leap, no longer pulls you back toward where you started.
  • Shadow Step's arrival stun now applies even if you give a move order right after landing.
  • An oversized gas mining prop was removed from the northwest room of the Abandoned Station.
  • Stances and Blazing Spikes are restored after a planned server restart, and your droid's cooldown is skipped when a planned restart boots you.
  • "Interrupted!" now floats when an interrupting attack lands.
  • Guard Reversal, Breaker Reversal, Charged Blows and the avoided-attack and Bleed-expiry stamina discounts now float a ready cue when they arm.
  • Enemies now notice a player who leaves stealth beside them, instead of ignoring you until you leave their range and return. Cloud Mind no longer works on players who are not in stealth.
  • Saber skills are removed from droid CPUs and the droid assembly window, since droids cannot use lightsabers. Existing parts keep working, and the four saber boost recipes are retired.
  • The kloo horn is held at the mouth while playing, and held horns now fit each body better. The Player Guide lists the playable instruments and which songs can be performed; the song picker stays disabled with a tooltip when only one song is available.
  • Standing up from the HNN Office benches no longer drops you into the black void, and five capstone guards (Tempest Bloom Stable Promoters in the Fight Club Backrooms and a Saber Cyclone Trial Examiner Droid in the Jedi Trial Halls) no longer stand out of reach in the void.
  • The black filler tiles around bunker, service duct and similar rooms no longer count as walkable floor, so you cannot walk into the void around them.
  • Ship modules that act on your own ship, such as the hypermatter injector and repairers, now work when an enemy beyond their range is selected, and Asteroids count as no target. Laser Cannon Battery no longer checks your selected target, and Turbolaser now needs a target.
  • Ship explosions now kill the crew aboard instead of players who already left, and logging out takes you off the ship's crew list.
  • Clicking a chat portrait to send a tell no longer refuses most nearby players: a doubtful tell shows who it reaches and a second click confirms it. Logins also take the connection slot that has been free longest instead of reusing one right away.
  • Clicking Send Tell in the Player List no longer leaves the window unresponsive until you relog. Window layout changes now wait until the press that triggered them ends.
  • Your Performance hotbar is kept until the restored hotbar is confirmed, so it is no longer lost if recovery is interrupted.
  • Droids no longer drop their rifle and fight unarmed when you are hit and give a command at the same moment.
  • Rain that starts while you are in an area now begins at full strength instead of staying a faint drizzle until you leave and return.
  • The flights terminals on the Smuggler's Moon landing pads are now named for Smuggler's Moon instead of Viscara.
  • Custom RGB colors, including RGB hair, now keep the same contrast as the matching palette color.
  • Female chest 078's jacket texture is fixed.
  • The Smuggler's Moon casino exit label is spelled correctly, and the Slums west and east exit labels now name the Hub and the Shipping District instead of both reading To Slums.
