Upcoming Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | October 31 Release Notes

This update opens the new planet Eshan, replaces the shield appearances with cleaner industrial designs, fills in missing Appearance Editor part pictures, and fixes hotbar overlap, Unarmed training, spawn placement, armor and staff models, and a login problem for some characters.

Eshan

  • Eshan, a cold, snowy world, is now open. Travel there from the galaxy map; its orbit has its own space area and its starport connects to the rest of the planet.
  • Explore Eshan City and its districts (Silver Gate, Highcrest Quarter, Hearth Ward, Verdant Crown, Riverway and the Old Quarter), plus the Keshan Heights and Peaks, Silverwood Expanse, Winter Marches, Veylan Groves, Shimmerdeep, Farmlands, Battlegrounds, Grand Proving Grounds, Scraplands, Shattervein, and the tunnels and caves beneath. A housing area and a spa are included, and Eshan has its own weather.
  • Eshan has its own enemies, a yeti boss, high-level resource spawns, and map pieces that drop from its creatures and rare enemies.
  • New quests include a series of hunting and security jobs (such as Break the Frost Pack, Thyrsian Incursion and Shatter Field Command), a Scraplands smuggler quest, and daily supply quests from Eshan City residents.
  • Two new tameable beasts live on Eshan: the Shattervein Wolf and the Echaolm.
  • New Echani cooking recipes and cold-weather field gear recipes can be learned and crafted.
  • Each Eshan area has an exploration achievement.

Skills

  • Bare-handed attacks now train Unarmed instead of Katar, including punches with no gloves or bracer equipped.

Appearances and Text

  • Shield appearances have been replaced with cleaner industrial designs. Your items keep their selected appearance.
  • The Appearance Editor now shows pictures for more body parts and armor pieces that were missing them.
  • Dyed metal on Republic Trooper armor shows its color correctly again.
  • The new staff parts no longer pinch where parts join.

Fixes

  • Extra hotbars can no longer overlap each other. A bar dragged, turned on or resized onto another moves to the nearest clear spot, so hovering and right-clicking always work.
  • Random creature and resource spawns no longer appear inside buildings and other structures, such as the burnt barn in Dantooine Ruined Farmlands where Thunes could trap themselves.
  • The shuttle status console no longer sits half inside the boarding bay wall.
  • Characters whose file names contain accented letters are now upgraded correctly on the server, so they are no longer refused at login.
