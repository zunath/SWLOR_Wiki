Upcoming Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | October 16 Release Notes

The next update brings a redesigned Player List, ship management and recovery improvements, dungeon balance and reward changes, a more compact DM toolbar, and fixes to combat, Mimicry, quests, language commands, equipment, appearances, and the game world. The release date and NWSync hash will be added once confirmed.

Combat and Mimicry

  • Overclocked Analyzer now boosts passive stat bonuses and resistances from equipped Mimicry traits during Overload.
  • Merciless Angle now displays its intended crimson slash effect on a successful hit instead of a cow animation.
  • Power Cell III now respects Self-Cast Support Abilities. With the setting enabled, it affects you and nearby allies without a target click. Turn it off to choose an ally manually.
  • Spears no longer receive the Single Weapon or Doublehand damage bonuses intended for one-handed weapons.

Player List

  • A redesigned Player List brings character names, descriptions, activity flags, tells, and party controls into one window.
  • The list respects the private names you assign with /name. Characters you have not named appear as Unassigned, with their public descriptions to help you recognize them.
  • Search and sort the list using the names and descriptions visible to you.
  • Choose activity flags for General RP, PvP, DM, and Crafting to show what you are interested in. The DM flag means you are looking for DM events, plots, or help with a scene. Each flag has its own icon and explanation.
  • Activity flags are saved separately for each identity and disguise.
  • Send a tell directly to the selected character, manage your party, or mark and clear hostility from the list. Party controls include inviting, removing, transferring leadership, and leaving.
  • DM characters are visible only to other DMs and are excluded from the player online count.

Ship Management

  • Ships left at the Abandoned Station return to their last regular starport after a server restart, and launch into the correct orbit.
  • Retrieving a player-built starport now returns ships docked there to a safe starport and the correct orbit.
  • The ship lists now reliably show all your owned and shared ships. The Other Ships list has page controls to make larger lists easier to browse.
  • Boarding a shared ship now requires Enter Property permission. Permission to rename or refit a ship does not grant boarding access.
  • Use Remove My Access to give up access to a shared ship and remove it from your list. Someone with permission to grant access must add you again if you want to return.

Great Arkanian Dragon

  • The Great Arkanian Dragon encounter and its rewards have received balance adjustments.
  • Arkanian Dragon crafting options have been expanded, with updates to existing equipment and recipes.

Abandoned Station

  • Colicoids replace the station's rakghoul encounters, including Subject Zero.
  • A Dock Master now provides access to your ship from the station. You must be out of combat to board.
  • Quest dialogue, journal entries, creature descriptions, and related item text have been updated to match.

Hutlar Droid Foundry

  • Adjusted enemy balance throughout the Foundry.
  • Chiro shards no longer drop in the Foundry, including from the Overseer.
  • Adjusted equipment reward balance, including a small reduction to Grapple Bracer's HP bonus.
  • Ranged droids now use proper firing animations.
  • Updated the appearances of the Coil Foreman and Salvage Inspector to match their ranged attacks.

Equipment Corrections

  • Affected cowls and headwraps now equip as helmets instead of gloves. Incorrectly classified gloves and hand coverings also use their proper equipment slots.
  • Hexweave Shawl now equips as a cloak, with an Attack bonus of 8 instead of 11.
  • Affected dungeon salvage visors now display as visors instead of a mask and hood.
  • Existing affected items update automatically, including stored items and droid equipment, while keeping their customizations and crafted enhancements. If the correct slot is occupied, the repaired item stays safely in inventory.
  • Existing crafted Arkanian equipment now receives the current equipment adjustments automatically while preserving enhancements.
  • Fixed some equipment stat adjustments being applied more than once during login.

Quests and Languages

  • Corrected outdated Vital Rupture references throughout the Viral Cascade capstone quests, including dialogue, journals, and related names. The final trial's text now correctly describes Viral Cascade's use of Venom and Infection.
  • The ancient sandworm no longer drops an extra stack of stolen goods. Each credited party member still receives the items needed to turn in the quest.
  • You can now select Nautila with either /language Nautila or /language Nautolan.

Music and Jukeboxes

  • Jukeboxes inside city buildings now use your music collection when you choose a song.
  • Apartment and ship jukeboxes use their current owner's collection. Jukeboxes placed directly in a city use its current mayor's collection.

Appearances

  • Updated affected weapons, armor, and helmets to appearances supported by the appearance editor. Existing copies receive the correction when the affected parts still use their original appearance; customized parts are preserved.
  • Restored smoother default body models for male characters using the human body set, with skin-color and clothing-dye customization supported.
  • Fixed missing or distorted lower legs on Miris Aven.
  • Affected male human shoulder styles now stay attached and move correctly with robe outfits.
  • Corrected Sharene's outfit in Hutlar Outpost.
  • Female thigh style 16 now uses the correct dye colors.
  • Fixed robe style 236's lower cloth getting stuck between the legs during movement and abilities on normal body types.
  • Transparent dye choices now correctly hide affected sections of cloak style 22 (Mantle).
  • Fixed helmets becoming invisible after selecting helmet style 64 in the appearance editor.

World and Housing

  • Corrected Crypt Marauder voices in the Sith Crypt Depths to match their Wookiee appearance.
  • Shuttle pilot droids now use droid voices.
  • Qion Slugs no longer catch clicks in empty space around their bodies, making it easier to select the ground nearby.
  • Fixed specialists in the Veles Militia Annex remaining stuck in place during combat.
  • Cleared debris blocking a doorway in Smuggler's Moon Station - Abandoned.
  • Enemies in the Fight Club Backrooms now spawn in reachable rooms rather than outside the playable area.
  • The unavailable furnished small Star Wars apartment has been removed from new rental and preview listings because it led to the wrong interior. Existing leases are preserved.
  • The jukebox in the Trade Concourse Smuggler Hideout now displays the name Jukebox.

DM Tools

  • The DM toolbar is more compact, with fully visible buttons, and no longer covers the inventory window's close button.
