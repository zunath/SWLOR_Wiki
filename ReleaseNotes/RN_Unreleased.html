Upcoming Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | October 18 Release Notes

The next update brings further Great Arkanian Dragon encounter adjustments, corrects Player List party status, fixes personal battle themes and stance animations, and improves clinic registration, dungeon exits, movement, and enemy spawns. The release date and NWSync hash will be added once confirmed.

Great Arkanian Dragon

  • The Great Arkanian Dragon encounter has received further balance adjustments, including a new Unnerving Roar ability and matching ability and status-effect icons.

Player List

  • Fixed characters appearing as Grouped in the Player List when they are not in a party.

Combat and Animations

  • Stances such as Cyclone, Imbuement, and Conduit now play an activation gesture when used unarmed or with a weapon that cannot show their usual animation. Other affected abilities receive the same correction.

Music

  • Battle themes selected in the Portable Music Player now play during combat and stop after the fight ends, including fights started with hostile abilities.

World and Travel

  • The exit from the Sith Fortress Lord's Quarters on Korriban no longer traps characters behind a locked door after a server restart. The dungeon's other key requirements remain unchanged.
  • Restored the Medical Registration Terminal at Veles Clinic on Viscara, allowing you to register it as your respawn point.
  • Removed invisible barriers blocking movement through parts of Smuggler's Moon Station - Lower Level.
  • Red Blades on Smuggler's Moon - Catwalks no longer spawn inside a structure where they cannot be reached.
