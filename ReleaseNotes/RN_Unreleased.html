Upcoming Release Notes

Back to Release Notes | November 19 Release Notes

This update adds DM-hosted events you can find, join and leave with /events, changes beast Focus Attention, fixes Eshan ship launches and skill coatings, moves old tunic listings to the market's Armor filter and stops the repeated rank limit warning.

Events

  • DMs can now host events. Type /events to see running events first, then upcoming ones with a countdown, start time, host and description.
  • When the host allows it, press Join to be sent to the event. Press Leave Event in the window or type /leaveevent to return to where you joined from. This still works after the event ends, after you log out and after a server restart.
  • Join and Leave are refused while you are dead, in combat or piloting a ship. You also cannot join from the out-of-character entry areas or while already at an event.
  • Event Announcements in General Settings is on by default and lets you turn off the announcement when an event starts.

Market

  • Tunic armor listed before the combat upgrade now appears under the market's Armor filter instead of under no filter. Newly listed tunics already did.

Beasts

  • Focus Attention now makes your beast's damage generate more Enmity (+15%, +30% and +50% at ranks I to III) instead of general Enmity, so Guarding Bond Stance no longer cancels it out. A Tank beast with Guarding Bond Stance now gets +100% Enmity from damage, matching player tank stances. Guarding Bond Stance's description now says its damage generates an additional +50% Enmity.
  • Creature beasts that spoke human voice lines or showed gibberish as chat text, such as the Graniteback Ursavar, now use fitting creature sounds.

World and Travel

  • The Flanged Jessoon now counts as an instrument for Play Instrument, with its own two-handed playing pose on every body.
  • Ships launched from Eshan now appear beside its dock instead of at the starlane arrival point. Ships already registered or docked there keep their old position.
  • Lightning no longer strikes players in Dantooine's Enclosed Mountain, the approach to the Jedi Enclave, or in Dathomir's Jungle Landing. Weather still plays, but you are protected from weather damage there.
  • Thunderstorm lightning no longer damages placeables that a DM placed for an event. It still hits creatures and other targets in range.
  • DMs have a new screen-shaking earthquake effect for events.
  • Fabrication can now craft 61 more indoor furnishings from the SWTOR set, including a range of beds and five jukeboxes, and you can place them in your property. The new seating is decorative only.
  • Fabrication has a new Statue of Darth Fomóíre recipe under Statues, unlocked at Fabrication rank 47, that makes a property decoration.

Appearances and Text

  • Black and very dark custom RGB dyes now stay matte and neutral instead of looking grey or tinted, such as near-black hoods looking grey and dark graphite looking green. Exact palette colors sent as RGB render as before.
  • Stoneskin, Greater Stoneskin, Barkskin, Petrify, Ice Skin and Shadow Shield coat your character again, including the lamp box on Havoc chest armor.
  • The Rustline Data Terminal now looks like a worn desk console that matches the other slicing structures, instead of a white information kiosk with a city advert. Terminals already in properties update after the next server restart.
  • Sixteen armor styles, including Robe 005, the respirators and Bicep 007, have been refitted to fit more playable bodies better.
  • The Ashmark Floor Mat bear rug no longer sinks into the floor on one side.
  • Deputy Kessa Vorn in Veles now wears a Veles Police uniform: black shirt with gold trim, sleeves rolled to the elbow, khaki trousers, and brown boots and belt.
  • Closing the Appearance Editor on the Wardrobe tab and opening it again no longer shows a red X on every body part picture and the part preview.
  • Chest 310 armor now deforms correctly on all four playable bodies, including the robe look.

Fixes

  • The Distribute RP XP window now rechecks its limits when you change a skill's Limit or Decay Lock in the Skills window, so it no longer keeps saying "Skill cap reached" until you close and reopen it.
  • A skill held at your own rank limit now warns once per login, the first time it would gain XP, instead of repeating the warning on every XP award. Each limited skill warns separately.
  • Random resource veins and slicing terminals no longer spawn in narrow passages, bridges, or corridor mouths where they could block your way. Resource counts and respawn timing are unchanged.
  • Tatooine's Rocky Pass now spawns random resource nodes like the other Anchorhead-region areas.
  • Removed an invisible wall on the open street beside the Cor Historic building near the crates and barrel in Veles.
  • Confirming removal of a market listing now removes the right listing, keeps your unsaved edits, and keeps the listing if your inventory is full.
  • Fixed errors with stale apartment and property storage selections and with category permissions on properties.
  • Fixed errors when browsing recipes, upgrading attributes, renaming, talking to quest NPCs, and with ship AI and docking when a record was missing.
  • The Mountain Jungles map now grants its key item correctly, and copies you already carry are repaired when used.
  • Auto-attacks now trigger splash damage such as Payload Pouch, and several other on-hit effects that were silently dropped during attacks now work: open character sheets refresh after on-hit stat changes, Standing Ovation heals when a hit drops you below 25% HP, leashing enemies keep their leash speed, removed slows and speed bonuses stop applying, and stealth and Espionage movement checks resume after an attack.
  • Completing an Engineering, Smithery, Fabrication or Agriculture guild task now puts a different task of the same rank on your board instead of the same task coming straight back.
  • Player houses, banks, city halls, medical centers, one apartment style and two starship interiors keep their daytime lighting at night instead of turning dark and blue.
  • Enemy attack telegraphs now fill in over their wind-up, so you can see how long is left before the attack lands. Previously only the outline showed.
  • The Piket and Ronto can no longer be hovered or clicked well outside their bodies.
  • Echani temple monks at the Silver Gate and on the Riverway ramp now walk through the doorways and arches instead of into the wall beside them.
