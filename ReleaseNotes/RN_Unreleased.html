This update replaces the shield appearances with cleaner industrial designs, fills in missing Appearance Editor part pictures, and fixes characters with accented letters in their file names being refused at login.

Appearances and Text

  • Shield appearances have been replaced with cleaner industrial designs. Your items keep their selected appearance.
  • The Appearance Editor now shows pictures for more body parts and armor pieces that were missing them.

Fixes

  • Characters whose file names contain accented letters are now upgraded correctly on the server, so they are no longer refused at login.
