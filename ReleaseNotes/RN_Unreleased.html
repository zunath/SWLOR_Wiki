Back to Release Notes | November 18 Release Notes

This update fixes the Prospecting and Search gathering windows, makes device pulses refund stamina, corrects status effect extensions and moves three capstone enemies onto open floor.

Gathering

  • The Prospecting window's text now wraps instead of being cut off, and the window grows with you when you enlarge it.
  • The Reaction, Line, Search, Plasma Forge, Kitchen, Circuit and Fabricator minigame windows wrap their long sentences instead of cutting them off, and grow with the window when you enlarge it.
  • The Search board's Search, Dig Deeper and Sift buttons stay clickable once a board is in play, so the first Search after choosing a spot is no longer dropped on a slow connection.

Fixes

  • Killzone Beacon, Blaster Beacon, Shock Beacon, Incendiary Field and Signal Jammer pulses now count for stamina hit rewards, such as Twin Blade's Sweeping Advance refund on area abilities.
  • Each furniture set's Field Cot now has a look of its own instead of the plain Cot.
  • Extending a status effect by a whole number of ticks, such as a 6 second Bleed extension on a 6 second interval, now adds exactly those ticks instead of one extra.
  • Three capstone enemies (the Trial Examiner Droid in the Jedi Trial Halls and the Stable Promoters in the Fight Club Backrooms) now stand on open floor instead of beside a wall or cliff edge.
