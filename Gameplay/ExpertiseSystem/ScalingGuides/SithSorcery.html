Sith Sorcery {Mentored}

Scaling Guide by QM Nick

What is Sorcery?

An arcane expression of Force ability first developed and practiced by the original Sith species through which they manipulated the power of the Dark Side. Practitioners combine Sith mysticism with their own experiments to create ever more strange rituals and alchemical sciences. Sith magic requires the cultivation of an intrinsic relationship with the Dark Side to produce its many and varied expressions and manifestations, though all of them twisted and evil. It's greatly aided by sorcerous implements such as ritualistic daggers and various talismans used for personal protection or as the focal point for a spell.

  • Danger: Failure when dealing with the Dark Side is an ever present threat as the power itself seeks to confuse and overwhelm those who would use it. The inattentive Sorcerer can easily become the victim of their own spell by so much as a wavering confidence or a misspoken word.

Relevant Feats

  • Necromancy {Rare, Mentored} (Sith Sorcery 1) - You have learned to how to bring life back to those who have died… or rather, unlife. Through rituals, you can create undead servants. The amount and strength of these servants increases as your expertise does.

Scaling

No points: This is a specific ritualistic practice which requires particular and deliberate connection with the Dark Side's corruptive energies. Sith are notoriously covetous of their knowledge, and none perhaps more so than Sorcerers who historically have gone to great lengths to conceal the scrolls of their writings.

  • Rituals: Anyone can serve as fodder, donating the energies of their lifeblood to a ritual.

 

1 Point: You have learned a selection of 'common', 'lesser' spells that are broadly shared by most Sorcerers to be utilised in combat. This includes abilities such as Force Blast, Bolt of Hatred, and Dark Web, as well as minor Illusions (hallucinations).

  • Rituals: Those initiated in sorcerous secrets have a supporting role in larger rituals, a step above acting as living batteries whose energies they will instead help shape and funnel to the leading Sorcerer.

 

2 Points: You have delved deeper into the ancient mysteries and begin to realise that Sorcery's true potential lies away from the scrum of battle, and is instead best used from a high and safe vantage point. Even so, your growing power in the Dark Side affords you greater precision and control over the sorcerous energies unleashed by those spells you do know, and a greater depth to the Illusions you can conjure, touching now all senses of your victim or producing a false aura.

  • Rituals: You can now lead your juniors and those newly initiated in known, well documented and time-tested rituals.

 

3 Points: Your experience serving at the foot of masters of the craft has given you ample knowledge and an intuition on how to craft your own vile experiments. Your power is now a serious imposition upon others, being able to wholly rob them of their senses and will, and supplant their thoughts and memories. (Requires consent or a QM/DM)

  • Rituals: You can devise and lead others into your own experimental rituals, able to work off of little more than partial records and descriptions. 

 

3☆ (Mastery): You've become one of the Dark Side's most potent conduits, deeply corrupted by its energies and unleashing that corruption wherever you go. Your vast library of ancient knowledge and intimate understanding of its contents allow you to devise and execute hexes and curses upon your enemies, picking out the right words and ingredients or suitable replacements under most any circumstance. Those dominated by your will may even be commanded to take their own lives (Requires consent or a QM/DM). The price for this depth of power is felt in your rapidly deteriorating body and flesh, hastening towards the inevitable.
