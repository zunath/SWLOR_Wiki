Enhance Attribute

Enhance Attribute represents your ability to use the Force to empower your body's natural physical capabilities to supernatural levels.

What can Enhance do, and/or what powers does it cover?

  • Force Speed, boosting your ability to move quickly - this is moving Fast. The most draining type of enhancement.
  • Boost your Agility to allow you enhanced coordination, flexibility, and general mobility - this is moving Well
  • Strengthen your muscles to achieve inhuman feats of physical strength and power
  • Force Jump, allowing you to leap much farther or higher than you normally could and withstand great falls
  • Art of Movement, enabling Force-boosted feats of free-running and parkour

What can Enhance *not* do?

  • Though it covers the force power of Inertia in theory, this cannot be used to alter your inertia in any sudden way such as to completely change direction mid-air

Scaling Guide

No Points: You can enhance your body's capabilities to a decent degree past what most non-Sensitives can achieve, but there are limits to your control and you become exhausted very quickly if you try to maintain active use. Big falls are scary and dangerous. You can generally enhance your capabilities in any given area by about 50%, and can only enhance yourself in one way at any given time. You can jump just a bit farther than a normal person.

 

1 Point: You have begun to learn to exercise more control over your self-enhancement and can maintain it for a bit longer, but using it still takes concentration. You cannot do much else through the Force while Enhancing yourself, and you can only reliably focus on enhancing one attribute at a time. You can enhance your Speed or Strength by about 100% (double the norm), and can withstand falls or jumps of a few meters without much fear.

 

2 Points: The Force flows through your body well. You have learned to channel it more freely and do not exhaust yourself as quickly, but extended use and particularly intense use will still drain you. At this stage you can begin to combine multiple aspects of enhancement together, such as Speed and Agility (moving fast AND with precision), or Strength and Jumping, but doing so is more draining than one at a time. You can enhance your Speed or Strength to about four times the norm. You can cushion falls of as high as a three-story building and jump up half that distance.

 

3 Points: Your capabilities with Force enhancement are strong indeed. While others may struggle with sustaining enhancement, you have become adept at keeping it up for significant periods of usage, although you are not entirely immune to wearing yourself out. Your Speed or Strength can be increased to roughly ten times the norm, and you can safely land from a height of roughly a hundred feet (30 meters) and jump half that distance in height or further horizontally with a running start.

 

3☆ (Mastery): Your body is a machine that turns Force into mind-blowing athleticism. You are capable of sustaining enhancement of your strength or agility more or less indefinitely, though only one of the two can be active at a time without starting to actually draw on your stamina. Your limits of strength and speed are entirely dependent on what a DM or QM will allow you to get away with - you are not the Flash or Superman.
