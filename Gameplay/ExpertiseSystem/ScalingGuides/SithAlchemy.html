Sith Alchemy {Mentored}

Scaling Guide by QM Nick

What is Sith Alchemy?

The science of imbuing items or living beings with the Dark Side of the Force, thereby permanently altering them. Like Sith Sorcery the applications are many and varied though generally categorized between non-living and living Alchemy. The former can be used for the production of such crafts as protective charms and talismans, Sith warblades, and Dark Armors. Living Alchemy meanwhile is primarily used to create Sithspawn beasts, though fleshcrafting can also be turned on sapient beings. It is also by far the more difficult discipline of the two, as the Living Force itself fights any who would twist its energies in such unnatural ways.

Relevant Feats

  • Mechu-Deru {Rare, Mentored} (Sith Alchemy 1 and Engineering 1) - You have learned to infuse machinery and computer systems with the Dark Side.
  • Alchemical Grafting (Sith Alchemy 2) - You can enable yourself or others to recover from serious injuries or limb loss by replacing the lost tissue, but the replacement is wrong somehow. Malformed, discolored, maybe even seeming like something that belongs on another creature.

Scaling

No points: There's a ritualistic element to the ancient Sith science and wrangling of the Dark Side such that it might imbue something to the practitioner's desired benefit is no mean feat even under the instruction of a master.

 

1 Point: You have been initiated in the deeper mysteries of the Sith, likely picking an avenue to focus your efforts whether in living or non-living Alchemy. You've learned what it takes to create the simpler tools of the trade such as the ubiquitous lesser Sith talismans, or how to induce individual mutations in animals and upkeep the existing beast pens of the Empire.

 

2 Points: You've gained plenty of experience under your belt and are ready to produce your own essays into the craft, be that bespoke arms and armor or more powerful Sithspawned beasts, whether a reproducing a proven design or iterating upon one.

 

3 points: Your renown as an Alchemist has grown commensurate to your dedication to the twisted science. The equipment you create is truly artisanal, serving you or those it is made for without fail. You are able to create the exact right beast for any problem you face and no challenge is too great, though twisting life is ever a fight against the natural flow of the Force.

 

3☆ (Mastery): The owner of your own beast pens where acolytes and the newly initiated upkeep the armies of Sithspawn you have created, your output is second only to a scant handful of individuals across the Empire in both volume and quality. Your mastery over the essence of life is beyond reproach, and the arms, armor, or greater talismans you can produce are the envy of any who would possess such a thing.
