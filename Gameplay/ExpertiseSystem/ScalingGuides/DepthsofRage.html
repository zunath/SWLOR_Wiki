Depths of Rage (AKA Force Rage)

Scaling Guide by QM Nick

The capacity and techniques by which a Force user can tap into their innermost fears, pain, and hate, and convert them into an intense rage. In such a state, the user finds themselves with increased speed, strength, and ferocity, and is able to fight through otherwise debilitating injury. However, the body can't handle such intense rage for long periods of time, greatly exhausting and weakening the user after the state subsides. Using this ability can permanently strain the wielder's connection to the natural flow of the Force, and push them inexorably towards the Dark Side.

Relevant Feats

  • Juggernaut (Depths of Rage 2 AND Endure Pain 2) - Through sheer rage and force of will, you can ignore otherwise debilitating injuries that most others would succumb to.

Scaling

No Points: You can be goaded to a rage and find yourself with an unexpected second wind, your physical attributes bolstered for a minute or so before succumbing to fatigue.

 

1 Point: You are able to sustain a state of rage for several minutes before becoming fatigued, though you still lack the requisite control needed to enrage yourself at will. Your speed and strength are notably but not overwhelmingly greater than is usual for you. This of course comes with a stronger pull to the Dark Side, and those new to its sway may well find the experience permanently damaging to their ego, psyche, and temperament.

 

2 Points: You are able to sustain your rage for long periods of time, significantly enhancing your physical attributes. Only those steeped in the Dark Side can achieve such a level of proficiency. You've learned exactly what makes you tick, and are able to bottle it to be unleashed on demand when the need is upon you.

 

3 Points: Tapping into your inner darkness and most potent emotions, you find yourself fighting with a great deal more fiery and controlled passion, offering greater resistance to the physical downsides of rage that you have become accustomed to in your journey down the dark path.

 

3☆ (Mastery): Your rage is a furnace, meticulously kindled and maintained so that it can never grow cold, and may roar to melting heat on demand. Your power allows you to transcend the frailty of the flesh, pushing past your physical limits, overcome the disabilities of old age, and even fading stamina. Yet always the candle that burns twice as bright burns half as long.
