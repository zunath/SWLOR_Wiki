The Expertise System

The Expertise system is a roleplay-centric framework through which characters learn and develop particular skills, abilities, and powers for use in roleplay scenarios and for benefits in GM-run events.

For a detailed explanation of the system and how it works, see this page:

Request Format

Submit a ticket once the character is eligible. Use this format:

Expertise/Feat Request
Character Name:
Expertise Ranks or Feats Requested:
RP Justification for Each Expertise, Feat, or Rank-Up:
Does your character have at least 200 total SP?:
Is the relevant skill rank 50?:

 

  • 200 SP Check: only required the first time the character requests expertise. Include a screenshot to display the SP total.
  • Expertise Requested: list up to 3 expertise ranks in the order you want them queued, including rank. Example: Pilot 1, Pilot 2, Diplomacy 1.
  • RP Justification: explain how the character has used, trained, or developed the skill in roleplay. Screenshots or logs are helpful but not required.

Justifications should focus on in-game roleplay rather than long backstory. Staff may deny requests that do not show enough active roleplay for the requested rank.

 

Forbidden / Banned Abilities

These are things that are considered too powerful or difficult to manage to place in Player hands without a vetting process that just isn't worth dealing with - or at all. These may appear as plot points or NPC abilities.

  • Force Sever
  • Psychometry
  • Force Phase
  • Nanotechnology
  • Force Storm (the wormhole one)
  • Teleportation
  • Anything that resembles Flight through the Force
  • Cloning

Miscellaneous Expertise

You may request an expertise that is not listed below, especially for RP-focused skills. The list is not exhaustive, but any expertise not listed here will require staff oversight to determine what precisely they govern and allow for.

The Dark Side and Corruption

Dark Side expertise must be portrayed as corruptive and dangerous. Powerful Dark Side abilities, including Sorcery, Alchemy, Lightning, and Drain, require appropriate roleplay around corruption, cost, and consequence. Characters cannot pursue or use the Dark Side as an objectively good or harmless path.

High-rank Dark Side practitioners are assumed to have practiced extensively. Consequences may be temporary or permanent depending on the character's actions and the story involved.

Common Available Fields of Expertise

Remember that this list is not comprehensive, and you are not limited to asking for or applying for fields of expertise listed here. If you want an expertise in something not present, simply create a ticket and ask, and Staff can work with you if it is something deemed suitable.

  • If an Expertise is labeled with the term ‘Category’ in the table below, this means that rather than being a single expertise, that section is referring to a number of separate Expertise fields that fall within the same general category.
  • If an Expertise is marked with a diamond (♦) symbol, players can freely select one of its listed Specializations when they first put a rank into it (so long as they meet its Restrictions) without expending a Feat slot. To add further Specializations beyond this, they must spend a Feat as normal.
  • In addition to being marked as such under the Restrictions column, Mentored and Rare expertise fields or specializations will be highlighted.
    • Mentored is Blue
    • Rare is Purple
    • Both is Orange
  • Expertise fields with an existing Scaling Guide will have a link to said guide in their name. These guides serve as explanatory breakdowns for various fields of expertise, including such information as what can and cannot be achieved with them, existing sub-feats and specializations, and a rough explanation of what is generally possible as you progress through ranks. Not all common expertise fields currently have scaling guides, but as new ones are written they will be added here.
