The Expertise System

The Expertise system is a roleplay-centric framework through which characters learn and develop particular skills, abilities, and powers for use in roleplay scenarios and for benefits in GM-run events.

For a detailed explanation of the system and how it works, see this page:

Expertise, Feats, & Mastery

The page is also available in Google Doc form here.

 

Scaling Guides

These guides serve as explanatory breakdowns for various expertises, including such information as what can and cannot be achieved with them, existing sub-feats and specializations, and a rough explanation of what is generally possible as you progress through ranks. Not all common expertises currently have scaling guides, but as new ones are written they will be added here.
